**Every sale has six steps. Write each step in the CRM.**

## The six steps

1. **Find** — a school owner or principal. A school group with branches is best.
2. **Talk** — ask the five questions below.
3. **Demo** — open balqalam.com on their phone and press "Try the platform".
4. **Free month** — we set up their school with them.
5. **Contract** — one page, signed by Abdout.
6. **Paid** — the first payment arrives.

## Five questions

1. How many students, and how many branches?
2. What do you use today?
3. What takes most of your time: fees, attendance, the timetable, parents?
4. Who decides, and when?
5. Can you start this month?

## Two WhatsApp messages

- **First:** "Hello, I'm [name] from Balqalam — one system for the whole school: admissions, timetable, attendance, live classes and fees, in Arabic. May I send you a link to try it? The first month is free."
- **Three days later:** "Hello [name], did you try the link? I can set up your school this week."

Send the Arabic version to Sudanese schools.

## Groups first

One contract with a school group brings many schools. Ask every school: do you have branches?
