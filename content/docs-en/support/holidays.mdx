## Steps

1. Open **School → Settings → Holidays**.
2. Under **Add a day off**, enter a name (for example Eid al-Fitr) and choose **Holiday** or **School closed**.
3. Choose the first day in **From**, and the last day in **Until** (leave it empty for a single day).
4. Click **Add**. To cancel one, click the bin next to it.

## Good to know

- On these days no live classes are created and no class reminders are sent, and the timetable says the school is closed.
- The timetable itself is kept; the days simply have no classes.

## Troubleshooting

- **Classes still appear on a holiday**: classes created before the holiday was added stay; add holidays ahead of time.
- **The wrong day was closed**: delete the entry and add it again with the right dates.
