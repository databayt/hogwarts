## Steps

1. Count the students enrolled in your school.
2. Add up the slices: **24 SAR for each of the first 250 students, 16 SAR for each of the next 250 (to 500), and 14 SAR for each after that.**
3. Read your annual price in the table below. Half is paid at signing, or when your free month ends. The rest is 4 instalments of 12.5%, the last one by the end of the school year.

## How the price works

You pay for the students you have, and the rate drops as the school grows. **Every new school starts with one free month.**

| Students | Rate per student a year |
| -------- | ----------------------- |
| 1–250 | 24 SAR |
| 251–500 | 16 SAR |
| 501–1,000 | 14 SAR |

| Students | Annual price | At signing (50%) | 4 instalments (12.5% each) |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | -------------------------- |
| 50 | 1,200 SAR | 600 SAR | 150 SAR |
| 100 | 2,400 SAR | 1,200 SAR | 300 SAR |
| 250 | 6,000 SAR | 3,000 SAR | 750 SAR |
| 300 | 6,800 SAR | 3,400 SAR | 850 SAR |
| 500 | 10,000 SAR | 5,000 SAR | 1,250 SAR |
| 1,000 | 17,000 SAR | 8,500 SAR | 2,125 SAR |

## What is included

- The full platform, hosting, and support by WhatsApp and email, Sunday to Thursday.
- Fixes and updates at no cost.
- Live classes up to fair use: 100 hours a month up to 250 students, 200 up to 500, 400 above. Extra hours are quoted.

## Good to know

- Schools in Saudi Arabia pay in riyals by bank transfer. There is no VAT line.
- Schools in Sudan pay the riyal price in **Sudanese pounds**, at the bank rate on the payment day, by Bankak.
- Over 1,000 students: 14 SAR for each extra student, with a dedicated contract. Email **sales@databayt.org**.
- Each payment uses the number of students on its date.

## Troubleshooting

- **You need an official quote or invoice** — email **sales@databayt.org** with your school's name and number of students.
- **Your student count changed** — nothing jumps. The next students are charged at the next rate, and each payment uses the students on its date.
