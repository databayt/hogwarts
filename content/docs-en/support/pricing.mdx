## Steps

1. Open the [price calculator](/pricing#calculator).
2. Set your number of students with the slider or type it in, then pick a currency: **SAR, USD, Sudanese pounds or Egyptian pounds**, at today's exchange rate.
3. Read your annual price: **(students − 100) × 24 SAR**. Half is paid at signing, and 12.5% at months 3, 6, 9 and 12.

## How the price works

Every student costs the same, **24 SAR a year** (about 2 SAR a month). **Your first 100 students are free, forever.** Each extra student adds exactly 24 SAR, with no bands and no jumps.

| Students | Annual price | At signing (50%) | Months 3, 6, 9, 12 (12.5% each) |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 100      | Free         | —                | —                               |
| 250      | 3,600 SAR    | 1,800 SAR        | 450 SAR                         |
| 500      | 9,600 SAR    | 4,800 SAR        | 1,200 SAR                       |
| 1,000    | 21,600 SAR   | 10,800 SAR       | 2,700 SAR                       |

## Good to know

- Every school starts with a **three-month free trial**.
- Included: the full platform, hosting managed by us, support by WhatsApp and email (Sunday to Thursday, working hours), and fixes and updates at no cost.
- Prices exclude VAT (15% in Saudi Arabia).
- Over 1,000 students: the same rate, with a dedicated contract and account manager. Email **contact@databayt.org**.
