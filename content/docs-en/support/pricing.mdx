## How the price works

Every student costs the same, **24 SAR a year** (about 2 SAR a month). That is 6,000 SAR for 250 students, divided per student. **Your first 100 students are free, forever.** So:

**Annual price = (students − 100) × 24 SAR**

| Students | Annual price | At signing (50%) | Months 3, 6, 9, 12 (12.5% each) |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 100      | Free         | —                | —                               |
| 150      | 1,200 SAR    | 600 SAR          | 150 SAR                         |
| 250      | 3,600 SAR    | 1,800 SAR        | 450 SAR                         |
| 300      | 4,800 SAR    | 2,400 SAR        | 600 SAR                         |
| 500      | 9,600 SAR    | 4,800 SAR        | 1,200 SAR                       |
| 1,000    | 21,600 SAR   | 10,800 SAR       | 2,700 SAR                       |

Each extra student adds exactly 24 SAR. There are no bands and no jumps. Use the [price calculator](/pricing#calculator) to see your school's price in **SAR, USD, Sudanese pounds or Egyptian pounds** at today's exchange rate.

## What's included

- The full platform for your enrolled students.
- Hosting: set up, managed and monitored by us.
- Support by WhatsApp and email, Sunday to Thursday during working hours.
- Platform faults fixed at no cost, with new versions and security fixes applied automatically.

## Good to know

- Every school starts with a **three-month free trial**.
- Prices exclude VAT (15% in Saudi Arabia).
- Over 1,000 students: the same rate, with a dedicated contract and account manager. Email **contact@databayt.org**.
