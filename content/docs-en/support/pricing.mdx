## Steps

1. Open the [price calculator](/pricing#calculator).
2. Set your number of students with the slider or type it in, then pick a currency: **SAR, USD, Sudanese pounds or Egyptian pounds**.
3. Read your annual price: **students × 24 SAR**. Half is paid when your free month ends, then 12.5% at months 3, 6, 9 and 12.

## How the price works

Every student costs the same: **24 SAR a year** (about $6.40, or 2 SAR a month). Each extra student adds exactly 24 SAR, with no bands and no jumps. **Every new school starts with one free month.**

| Students | Annual price | First payment (50%) | Months 3, 6, 9, 12 (12.5% each) |
| -------- | ------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 50       | 1,200 SAR    | 600 SAR             | 150 SAR                         |
| 100      | 2,400 SAR    | 1,200 SAR           | 300 SAR                         |
| 250      | 6,000 SAR    | 3,000 SAR           | 750 SAR                         |
| 500      | 12,000 SAR   | 6,000 SAR           | 1,500 SAR                       |

## Good to know

- Schools in Sudan pay the **US-dollar price in Sudanese pounds**, at the bank rate on the payment day, by bank transfer.
- Included: the full platform, hosting, support by WhatsApp and email, and fixes and updates at no cost.
- Prices are before tax.
- Over 1,000 students: the same rate, with a dedicated contract. Email **sales@databayt.org**.

## Troubleshooting

- **You need an official quote or invoice** — email **sales@databayt.org** with your school's name and number of students.
- **Your student count changed** — each payment uses the number of students on its date.
