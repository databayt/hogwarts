## Steps

1. Open your school's login page and enter your email or username and your password.
2. Click **Login**.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot Password?** and enter your email.
4. Open the link we email you and set a new password.

## Good to know

- Password reset works by email only. If you sign in with a username and have no email, ask your school admin to **Generate Credentials** again.
- People added by a bulk import choose their own password the first time they sign in.
