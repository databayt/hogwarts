## Steps

1. Open your **school's own address**, for example `yourschool.balqalam.com`, and go to the login page. A login only works at the school that created it.
2. In **Email or username**, type your email, or the 6-digit login code the school gave you.
3. Type your **Password** and click **Login**. Your **Dashboard** opens. If you see the school's public home page instead, open the **☰** menu and tap **Platform**.
4. First time in with the PIN from the school? Balqalam asks you to **Choose your own password**: type a new one twice (4 digits is enough) and click **Save and continue**.
5. Forgot your password and you have an email? Click **Forgot Password?**, enter your email, and open the reset link we send you.
6. Forgot it and you sign in with a 6-digit code (no email)? Ask the school office. An admin opens your row in **Students**, **Teachers**, **Parents** or **Staff** and clicks **Generate Credentials**. That gives you a new PIN on the spot.

## Good to know

- The login code is 6 digits and the school's PIN is 4 digits. Every person in a school has their own code.
- Want to change your email? Ask the school office to update it on your account.
- You can change your password any time in **Settings → Password**.
- When an admin resets your password, any lockout is lifted at the same time.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Account not found"**: type the 6-digit login code in the first box exactly as you received it, not your name or phone number. If it keeps happening, your account hasn't been created yet, so ask the school office to **Generate Credentials**.
- **The code works but my email doesn't**: your email may not be on your account. Sign in with the code and ask the office to add your email.
- **The box looks filled but says "required"**: the browser is suggesting a saved email without typing it. Tap the box and type it yourself.
- **"Access Denied"**: you are signed in with an account that doesn't belong to this school. Click **Logout** in the menu, then sign in with your code.
- **"Too many wrong attempts"**: after 5 wrong tries the login is blocked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask the school office to reset your password, which unblocks you straight away.
- **"Wrong username, email or password" with the right password**: check that you are on your own school's address and not balqalam.com or another school's, and that the code has no spaces.
- **The reset email never arrives**: check spam, and wait a few minutes. We only send it if that email is on an account. If you sign in with a code, ask the school office instead.
- **"This reset link has expired"**: click **Forgot Password?** again and use the newest email.
- **"This account is suspended"**: only the school can lift this. Contact the school office.
