## Steps

1. Open the **app link** your school sent you, or the one on your school's sign-in page, on an Android phone, and download the file.
2. If the phone asks, allow installing from this source, then tap **Install**.
3. Open the app and allow the microphone, camera and notifications when it asks.
4. Sign in with your **username** (the number on your card) and password, not your email.
5. When **Update required** appears, tap **Update now** and wait. If you are in a live class, the app waits until you leave and then asks.

## Good to know

- The app carries your school's name and colors. It is installed from your school's link, not from the Play Store, so the phone may show a warning. The app is safe.
- Keep **one copy** of the app on your phone. An old icon (such as the orange website icon) causes confusion. Long-press it and choose **Uninstall**.
- iPhone and computers need no app: open your school's address in the browser. See [Mobile and offline](/docs/support/mobile).
- On Android, classes open in the app only. If you open a class in the browser it shows **Open the app** or **Get the app**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"App blocked to protect your device" or "harmful app"**: this is normal because the app is not in the store. Do not tap **OK**; tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
- **It still refuses to install**: open **Play Store ← your picture ← Play Protect ← ⚙️** and turn off **Scan apps**, install, then turn it back on. On Samsung turn off **Auto Blocker** in Settings.
- **The link won't open**: turn off the VPN, or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, then try again.
- **I can't install the app**: tap **I couldn't install the app** on the class page. You get in through the browser for that day only, and the class supervisor is told.
- **The app shows another school, classes that are not yours, or closes after the logo**: uninstall it (long-press, **Uninstall**), download it again from your school's link, and sign in with your username.
- **I need the app link**: ask the class supervisor or the school administration, or ask the assistant.
