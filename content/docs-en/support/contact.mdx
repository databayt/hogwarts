## Steps

1. Email **contact@databayt.org**.
2. Include your school's name, what went wrong and a screenshot.
3. It also helps to tell us the page you were on, what you clicked, and any error message.

## Good to know

- Support runs by WhatsApp and email, Sunday to Thursday during working hours.
- Check the [common questions](/docs/support/faq) first. The answer may already be there.
