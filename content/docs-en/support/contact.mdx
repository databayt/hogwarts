## Steps

1. Email **contact@databayt.org**.
2. Include your school's name, what went wrong and a screenshot.
3. It also helps to tell us the page you were on, what you clicked, and any error message.

## Good to know

- Support runs by WhatsApp and email, Sunday to Thursday during working hours.
- Check the [common questions](/docs/support/faq) first. The answer may already be there.

## Troubleshooting

- **No reply yet** — we answer Sunday to Thursday during working hours. Check your spam folder, then write again with the same subject.
- **It's urgent, everyone is locked out** — say so in the subject line and include your school's web address.
- **You can't take a screenshot** — on a computer press **PrtSc** (Windows) or **Shift + Cmd + 4** (Mac). On a phone press the power and volume-up buttons together.
