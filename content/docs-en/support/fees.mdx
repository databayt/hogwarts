## Steps

1. **School fee**: set it once in **School → Configuration → Price**, or in the price step of onboarding. Every grade starts from it.
2. **Grade fee**: open **Finance → Fees → Structure**, open a grade's fee and save a new amount. The grade is now **Fine-tuned for this grade**. **Reset to school fee** puts it back.
3. **Student price**: open **Finance → Fees → Assignments**, open the student's fee, and under **Student price** click **Save price**. **Reset to grade fee** puts it back. A **Discount** with a reason goes on the same card.
4. Students placed in a grade get that grade's fee automatically, and invoices are created from it.
5. Record a payment under **Payments**, or let parents pay online. Share an invoice by link, PDF or email.

## Good to know

- Prices flow school → grade → student. A change only reaches the levels still inheriting it. Fine-tuned grades and students with their own price keep theirs.
- Anything already paid is never re-priced. Open instalments are re-split so they add up to the new amount.
- Moving a student to another grade, or promoting them, cancels this year's unpaid old-grade fee and assigns the new one.
- Parents pay by card, cash, bank transfer or mobile wallet (Bankak/mBOK). They see their fees under **Finance**, and get a daily reminder when a fee is overdue.

## Troubleshooting

- **I changed the school fee but a grade didn't move**: that grade is fine-tuned. Open it and click **Reset to school fee**.
- **One student kept the old price**: they have their own price. Open their fee and click **Reset to grade fee**.
- **A student still shows their old grade's fee**: change the grade from the student's record, not the fee. This year's unpaid old fee is then cancelled for you.
- **A paid fee didn't change after re-pricing**: that's on purpose. Paid amounts are never rewritten. Add a fine or a discount instead.
- **"Enter a valid amount"**: type digits only, without commas or a currency sign.
