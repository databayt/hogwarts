## Steps

1. Open **School → Security** to see your **Security Score**, **Failed Logins** in the last day, and how many users have **2FA Enabled**.
2. Open the **Audit Log** tab to see who did what, and **Access Control** for who can reach what.
3. To export a list, open it (for example **Students**) and click **Export CSV**.

## Good to know

- Each school's data is kept apart: every record belongs to its school, and each school has its own address.
- Every user sees only what their role allows.
- Sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256.
- For hosting, backup or contract questions, email **contact@databayt.org** and the team will confirm them for you.

## Troubleshooting

- **Many failed logins** — someone may be guessing a password. Ask that user to reset their password, and **Suspend** the account in **Membership** if you don't recognise it.
- **A staff member left the school** — **Remove** or **Suspend** them in **Membership** so they can no longer sign in.
- **You think someone saw data they shouldn't** — check the **Audit Log**, then email **contact@databayt.org** with the time and the page.
