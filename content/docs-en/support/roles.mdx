## Steps

- **Admin**: everything: school, students, teachers, admission, finance and settings.
- **Teacher**: students, classrooms, exams, attendance, timetable and grades.
- **Student**: their own attendance, exams, grades, timetable and assignments.
- **Parent**: the parent portal, grades, exams and attendance.
- **Accountant**: finance, admission and transport fees.
- **Staff**: students, teachers, parents and admission.
- **Everyone** also sees the overview, announcements, library, profile and settings.

## Good to know

- Every user sees only what their role allows.
- Give teachers and parents a login with **Generate Credentials**, or hand out logins after a bulk import.
