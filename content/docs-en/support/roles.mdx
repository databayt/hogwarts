## Steps

1. Open **School → Membership** to see everyone in your school, with their role and status.
2. To change someone's role, open the row's actions, choose **Change Role**, pick the **New Role** and save.
3. To block someone for now, choose **Suspend**. **Activate** gives their access back. **Remove** unlinks them from the school.
4. New people who asked to join wait under **Pending Requests** until you approve them.

## Good to know

- **Admin**: everything: school, students, teachers, admission, finance and settings.
- **Teacher**: students, classrooms, exams, attendance, timetable and grades.
- **Student**: their own attendance, exams, grades, timetable and assignments.
- **Parent**: the parent portal, grades, exams and attendance.
- **Accountant**: finance, admission and transport fees.
- **Staff**: students, teachers, parents and admission.
- **Everyone** also sees the overview, announcements, library, profile and settings.
- Every user sees only what their role allows.
- Give teachers and parents a login with **Generate Credentials**, or hand out logins after a bulk import.

## Troubleshooting

- **A teacher can't see a page** — the page belongs to another role, for example **Finance** is for admins and accountants. Change the role in **Membership** if they need it.
- **A role change doesn't show** — ask the person to sign out and sign in again.
- **Someone can't sign in at all** — check **Membership**: a **Suspended** member is blocked until you click **Activate**.
- **A new member sees nothing** — their request may still be under **Pending Requests**. Approve it.
