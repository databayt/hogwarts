## Steps

1. Open **Live**, then **Sessions**, and click **+** (Create Live Class).
2. **Basics**: pick the **Class** from your timetable; the subject, class, teacher and time fill in by themselves. Change the **Title** if you like, such as "Unit 1 review", and click **Next**.
3. **Schedule**: check the date, start and end time, and click **Next**.
4. **Meeting**: keep **In-app room** so the class is recorded and attendance is taken for you, or pick **External link** and paste a Google Meet, Zoom or Teams link.
5. **References**, which is your lesson prep: link the curriculum **Lesson**, so its files become **slides** in class, and attach an **Exam / Quiz**, an **Assignment** and a **Quick check** to launch during class, plus a reference link if you have one.
6. **Access**: choose **Section only (private)** or **Whole school**, and click **Create Live Class**.
7. At class time open **Timetable** and click the period that is running (or up to ten minutes before), which takes you to its room.
8. Click **Join** and allow the browser your camera and microphone. You host the room: the whiteboard, slides and screen sharing are in the bottom bar.

## Good to know

- A period from the timetable gets its room on its own when you click it at class time, so you only need the wizard to prepare resources or for a one-off class.
- To edit a scheduled class: in **Sessions**, open the row's menu and click **Edit**.
- After class, set homework from the class page with **Set homework**.

## Troubleshooting

- **No "slides" in class**: no curriculum lesson was linked in **References**, or the lesson has no files. Edit the class and link one.
- **My periods aren't in the "Class" list**: the school hasn't assigned your subjects or entered the timetable yet.
- **"Join" doesn't open the room**: allow the camera and microphone from the browser's address bar, and try **Network test** in **Live**.
