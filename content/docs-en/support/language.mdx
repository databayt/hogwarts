## Steps

1. Click the languages icon in the top bar (**Switch language**).
2. The platform switches between Arabic and English, with its direction: Arabic right to left, English left to right.

## Good to know

- Arabic is the default. On a first visit, Balqalam follows your browser's language.
- Your choice is remembered on that device for a year.
- Names and the details you type stay as you wrote them. Only the platform's buttons and labels change.

## Troubleshooting

- **It keeps opening in the wrong language** — switch once more from the top bar. Clearing the browser's cookies resets the choice.
- **Some words still show in English** — a few screens are not fully translated yet. Email **contact@databayt.org** with a screenshot so we can fix it.
- **A different phone shows the other language** — the choice is saved per device. Switch it there too.
