## Steps

1. Click the round assistant button at the bottom corner of any page.
2. Pick one of the suggested questions, or type your own, in Arabic or English.
3. The answer appears as it is written. Press the square **Stop** button to cut it short.
4. Under a how-to answer you get the matching guide, its tutorial video and screenshots. Open them right inside the chat.
5. Ask a follow-up such as "and after that?". The assistant keeps the topic you were on.
6. Press the **menu** button at the top of the chat to see your earlier chats, open one, rename it or delete it. The pen button starts a new chat.

## Good to know

- Inside your school's dashboard, the assistant knows your role, so a teacher and a parent get the guide written for them.
- Ask "what can you help with?" to see every guide it can walk you through.
- You can send a few messages a minute. If you see "too many messages", wait a moment.
- Your chats are saved so you can come back to them. Signed in, they are kept with your account at this school until you delete them. On a public page without signing in, they are kept on this device and deleted automatically 90 days after your last message.
- Your chats are yours: other members of your school can't see them. Delete one chat, or all of them, from the menu. On a public page, **Forget this device** deletes them right away.
- To write an answer, your question is sent to an AI service. Don't type passwords, PINs or login codes into the chat.

## Your chats and privacy

- **What is kept:** your questions, the answers, and the guide each answer used.
- **Signed in:** kept with your account, for this school only, until you delete them.
- **Not signed in:** kept for this browser through a cookie, and deleted 90 days after your last message. Another school's site doesn't see them.
- **Deleting:** open the menu in the chat. Delete one chat, all of them, or, when you aren't signed in, **Forget this device**.
- **Contact details:** if you write your email or phone number in the chat on balqalam.com, our team uses it to reply to you.

## Troubleshooting

- **No video or screenshots under the answer.** Not every guide has a video yet. Use the guide link instead.
- **"Sign in to ask the support assistant."** Inside a school dashboard, the assistant answers signed-in members only. Sign in again.
- **The answer doesn't fit your problem.** Use **Report an issue** at the bottom of the page, or [contact support](/docs/support/contact).
- **Your earlier chats are missing.** Chats are kept per school and per bot. Signed out, they belong to the browser you used; a different browser or a cleared cookie starts fresh.
