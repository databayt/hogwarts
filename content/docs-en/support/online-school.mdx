## Steps

1. Open **School → Settings → Teaching mode** and choose how classes run:
   - **In person**: every class meets in the building. There are no live rooms or join buttons.
   - **Live**: every class is also taught live. Students and teachers join it from the timetable.
   - **Both**: in person and live together. You choose which grades and sections go live, or open a temporary online window.
2. Open **Live** from the sidebar. The **School readiness for online teaching** panel shows what works now and what still needs setting up.
3. If it says some weekly periods have **no teacher**, click **Assign teachers**. A period with no teacher has no host, so it never becomes a live class. On **Assign teachers**, click a **No teacher** cell and choose the teacher for that subject and section.
4. Declare your days off under **School → Settings → Holidays**, so no classes are created and no reminders are sent on those days.
5. Add your students and their guardians (see [Add a student](/docs/support/add-student)), and share their login details.
6. That's it. Each school day the classes of the timetable are created as live classes on their own. Students and teachers join by clicking the class in their timetable, or from the card on their home page.

## Good to know

- Live classes are added to the class, never instead of it: the classroom stays on the timetable either way.
- **School → Settings → Live classes** holds the detailed options: which grades and sections go live, automatic attendance, whether guardians may watch, room tools, reminders, and recording.
- Turn on **Auto-mark attendance** there if you want attendance written from who joined each class.
- Going online for a few days (bad weather, an emergency)? Choose **Both** and open a temporary online window in **Live classes**. It ends on its own.

## Troubleshooting

- **No live classes appear today**: check the teaching mode is **Live** or **Both**, today is not a declared holiday, and the period has a teacher.
- **The readiness panel says the meeting platform needs setting up**: contact us; in-app rooms are enabled per school.
- **A class shows but nobody can enter**: the teacher starts the class; students can enter once it has started.
