## Steps

1. Open **Attendance** from the sidebar. As a teacher you land on **Quick Attendance**.
2. Choose your section. The class you teach now is marked **Now**.
3. Everyone starts as present. Tap a student once for **absent**, again for **late**, and a third time for **present** again.
4. Click **Save attendance**. Guardians of absent students are notified automatically.
5. Want another way? Open the **Take attendance** tab and pick a method:
   - **Class list**: one section and day, with **Mark all present** and the keyboard shortcuts P, A and L.
   - **QR code**: show a code on the board. Students scan it from the school app, and it refreshes on its own.
   - **ID cards**: scan a student's card, or type its number.
   - **More → Self check-in kiosk**, **Upload a file** (CSV or Excel, one section and one day, from the template) or **Hall passes**.

## Good to know

- Quick Attendance works offline. With no connection it says **Attendance saved on this device**, and it syncs and notifies guardians when you're back online.
- Parents submit excuses from the parent portal, and you review them in the **Excuses** tab.
- Admins also see **Students at risk**, **Reports** and **Settings**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No sections assigned to you yet"**: you aren't set as the teacher of any section. Ask an admin to assign you to your sections.
- **"No school today"**: today isn't a school day in the school calendar, so attendance isn't expected.
- **"Failed to save attendance"**: check your connection and click **Save attendance** again. On a phone it saves on the device and syncs later.
- **Upload says some rows had issues**: download the template again, keep its columns, and upload only one section and one day per file.
- **A card says "This card isn't registered"**: add the card under **ID cards → Cards** first.
