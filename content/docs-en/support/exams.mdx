## Steps

1. Open **Exams** from the sidebar. Click **Create Exam** on the overview, or the round **+** button in **Manage**.
2. Choose how to start: **Start from a template** (fastest), **Generate with AI**, or **Or schedule a blank exam**.
3. Fill in the exam: **Basic Information** (title, class, subject, type), **Schedule & Marks** (date, duration, total and passing marks), then **Instructions & Details**.
4. Keep your questions in **Qbank**: click **Add Question**, or import them, tagged by subject, type and difficulty.
5. After the exam, open **Mark**. Multiple choice, true/false and fill-in-the-blank mark themselves; type the rest under **Enter marks** and click **Save Marks**, or use **Import CSV**.
6. Click **Auto-mark & publish**. Students are notified and the marks go into the gradebook.

## Good to know

- For essays and short answers, the AI suggests a mark. The teacher can change any mark before and after publishing.
- The exam paper downloads as a Word file filled into the school's own template (**Templates**).
- Students see **Upcoming**, take on-screen exams with a timer, and see their results under **Record**. Parents see their child's results.
- Scheduling clashes show on the exam calendar as **Schedule conflict detected**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Marks cannot exceed …"** — the mark is above the exam's total. Fix the mark, or raise **Total Marks** on the exam.
- **A student is missing from the marks list** — the student must be in the exam's class. Check the student's grade and section, then reload **Mark**.
- **The paper has fewer questions than planned** — the question bank is short for that type or difficulty. Add questions in **Qbank**, then generate again.
- **Students can't see their results** — results appear only after **Auto-mark & publish**.
- **The CSV import is rejected** — start from **Download CSV Template** and keep its columns and student IDs.
