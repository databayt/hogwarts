## Steps

1. Open **Missed classes**: from the strip at the top of the dashboard «Missed classes: … · **Make them up**», or from **Live** → **Missed classes**.
2. Tap **Make up this class** next to the missed class. The **Make-up class** sheet opens.
3. Pick the time one of three ways:
   - **Earliest time free for the teacher and the section** → **Book this time**.
   - Or **Pick a day**, then **Pick a time** from the free slots.
   - Or **Another time** → type the **Date** and **Start time** yourself. This way you can make up on **any day, even one you don't normally teach**.
4. Tap **Confirm make-up class**. Students and parents are notified, and reminded ten minutes before.

## Good to know

- If a teacher hasn't entered their class five minutes after it starts, the class supervisor and the administration get an alert, and the supervisor can book a **make-up class** or enter as the class teacher.
- If a make-up class is moved, everyone is notified of the new time.
- The time shown is school time, even for people abroad.
- A missed class that was recorded appears with its recording if the school records classes.

## Troubleshooting

- **There is no "Book" button**: the class hasn't been missed yet, or it isn't your class. Ask the class supervisor.
- **The suggested time doesn't suit me**: pick another free time before confirming.
- **I didn't get a make-up class notification**: turn on notifications on your phone. See [Notifications](/docs/support/notifications).
