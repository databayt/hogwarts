## How do I start my school on Balqalam? [#get-started]

Click **Get Started** on balqalam.com and follow the setup wizard, which takes 10–30 minutes. [Getting started](/docs/support/get-started)

## How much does it cost? [#pricing]

24 SAR per student a year, and your first 100 students are free forever. [Pricing and payment](/docs/support/pricing)

## How do I sign in, or reset a forgotten password? [#login]

Sign in with your email or username, and use **Forgot Password?** to get a reset link by email. [Signing in and passwords](/docs/support/login)

## Who can see what? [#roles]

Each role sees its own pages: admins see everything, while teachers, students, parents, accountants and staff see only their part. [Roles and permissions](/docs/support/roles)

## How do I switch between Arabic and English? [#language]

Click the languages icon in the top bar (**Switch language**). [Arabic and English](/docs/support/language)

## How do teachers take attendance? [#attendance]

Open **Attendance**, tap only the absent students in **Quick Attendance**, and save. [Attendance](/docs/support/attendance)

## How do I set up and collect fees? [#fees]

Create a fee under **Finance → Fees → Fee Structures**, then assign it to students. [Fees and invoices](/docs/support/fees)

## Does Balqalam handle timetables, exams and grades? [#academics]

Yes: weekly timetables, a question bank and online exams, report cards and transcripts. [Timetable, exams and grades](/docs/support/academics)

## How do parents get a login? [#parents]

Use **Generate Credentials** on the student, import guardians in bulk, or link the parent with an access code. [Parents](/docs/support/parents)

## Is there a mobile app, and does it work offline? [#mobile]

Balqalam installs on any phone from the browser, and Quick Attendance and Lumos lessons work offline. [Mobile and offline](/docs/support/mobile)

## Is our school's data safe? [#security]

Each school's data is kept apart, every user sees only what their role allows, and sensitive data is encrypted. [Data security and privacy](/docs/support/security)

## How do I contact support? [#contact]

Email **contact@databayt.org** with your school's name, what went wrong and a screenshot. [Contacting support](/docs/support/contact)
