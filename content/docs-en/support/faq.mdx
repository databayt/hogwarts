## Signing in and passwords [#login]

- Sign in on your school's login page with your email or username, then click **Login**.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot Password?** and enter your email. You'll get a link to set a new one.
- Password reset works by email only. If you sign in with a username and have no email, ask your school admin to **Generate Credentials** again.

## Roles and permissions [#roles]

| Role       | Sees                                                                 |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Admin      | Everything: school, students, teachers, admission, finance, settings |
| Teacher    | Students, classrooms, exams, attendance, timetable, grades           |
| Student    | Their own attendance, exams, grades, timetable and assignments       |
| Parent     | The parent portal, grades, exams and attendance                      |
| Accountant | Finance, admission, transport fees                                   |
| Staff      | Students, teachers, parents, admission                               |

Everyone also sees the overview, announcements, library, profile and settings.

## Arabic and English [#language]

Click the languages icon in the top bar (**Switch language**) to switch between Arabic and English. Arabic, written right to left, is the default, and your choice is remembered for a year.

## Attendance [#attendance]

1. Teachers open **Attendance** and land on **Quick Attendance**, where everyone starts as present.
2. Tap only the students who are absent, then save.

Parents of absent students are notified automatically and can submit an excuse. You can also take attendance manually or with QR codes, barcodes, a kiosk, a CSV upload or a geofence. Quick Attendance works offline and syncs when the connection returns.

## Fees and invoices [#fees]

1. Go to **Finance → Fees → Fee Structures** and create a fee: amount, category, grade, number of instalments and late fee.
2. Assign it to students one by one or in bulk. A student placed in a grade gets that grade's fees automatically.

Parents can pay by card (Stripe), cash, bank transfer or mobile wallet (Bankak/mBOK), and a daily reminder goes out for overdue fees. Invoices are created automatically from fees, or by hand under **Invoice**. Share them by link, PDF or email.

## Timetable, exams and grades [#academics]

- **Timetable**: a weekly schedule for each section, made by hand or generated automatically, with conflict checks and PDF export.
- **Exams**: a question bank, generated exam papers, online exams, automatic marking and certificates.
- **Grades**: report cards, transcripts, promotion and report-card templates.

## Parents [#parents]

- Adding a student saves the parent as a contact. To give a parent a login, use **Generate Credentials**, import guardians in bulk, or link them with an access code.
- The **parent portal** shows their children, fees, announcements, events and messages, plus the children's attendance, grades and exams.

## Mobile and offline [#mobile]

- Balqalam installs as an app, with your school's name and colours.
- **Android (Chrome)**: open your school's address and tap **Continue** to install.
- **iPhone (Safari)**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- Quick Attendance and Lumos lessons keep working offline and sync later. Other pages need a connection.

## Data security and privacy [#security]

- Each school's data is kept separate: every record belongs to its school, and each school has its own address.
- Every user sees only what their role allows.
- Sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256.
- Lists export to CSV with the **Export CSV** button.

For hosting, backup or contract questions, email the team.

## Contacting support [#contact]

Email **contact@databayt.org** with your school's name, what went wrong and a screenshot. It also helps to tell us the page you were on, what you clicked, and any error message.
