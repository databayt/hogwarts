## Steps

1. Open **Timetable** from the sidebar. Admins see the whole school's week grid, with filters by class and teacher.
2. Set the school day once in **Timetable → Settings**: **Working Days**, the periods and **Break & Lunch**.
3. To build the week automatically, open **Generate**, choose the term, check the **Hard Constraints** and **Workload Limits**, then click **Generate Preview**.
4. Review the preview. Click **Merge** to keep your current lessons and fill the gaps, or **Replace All** to start fresh.
5. To change one lesson, click its cell in the grid, pick the subject, teacher and room, and save.
6. Open **Conflicts** and click **Scan for Conflicts** to find any teacher or room booked twice in the term.

## Good to know

- Every school starts with a week already filled from its grades and subjects. A slot with no free teacher shows as **Unassigned**.
- Saving a lesson is refused if it would put a teacher, a room or a class in two places in the same period.
- Teachers see their own teaching week (**Today** and **Week View**), students see their class's week, and parents see each child's week.
- Print the timetable on A4 from the browser.
- Who teaches what comes from the **Teachers** tab and each teacher's subjects.
- When the school teaches live, click today's class in the timetable while it runs (or up to 10 minutes before) to join it. A teacher's click starts the class.

## Troubleshooting

- **A lesson shows Unassigned** — no teacher of that subject was free. Give a teacher that subject in their **Expertise**, then generate again with **Merge**.
- **"Generation Partial" or Unplaced Classes** — check teacher availability and room capacity, or relax **Max Periods/Day** in **Workload Limits**, then **Regenerate**.
- **"Conflict Detected" when saving** — the teacher or room is already booked in that period. Pick another period or teacher, or fix it from **Conflicts**.
- **A teacher can't see their timetable** — the teacher must be assigned to the lessons. Check the **Teachers** tab, then ask them to reload.
- **The wrong days or periods appear** — fix **Working Days** and the periods in **Timetable → Settings** for the right term.
