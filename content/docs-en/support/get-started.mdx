Every school gets a full **three-month free trial** with no fees and no commitment. After it, the first 100 students stay free forever.

## Steps

1. On [balqalam.com](https://balqalam.com), click **Get Started**. Sign in, or create an account if you don't have one.
2. **Basic information**: your school's name, a short description and its location.
3. **Setup**: capacity, branding and logo. If you like, import your students from a CSV or Excel file now. You can also do it later from [bulk import](/docs/support/bulk-student).
4. **Business**: visibility, price, terms, and your school's web address, for example `yourschool.balqalam.com`.
5. Accept the terms. The school is activated, with its default grades, departments, terms and roles ready to use.

Setup usually takes 10–30 minutes. Next steps: [add a student](/docs/support/add-student) or [import a whole class](/docs/support/bulk-student).
