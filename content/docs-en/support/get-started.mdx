## Steps

1. On [balqalam.com](https://balqalam.com), click **Get Started**. Sign in, or create an account if you don't have one.
2. **Basic information**: your school's name, a short description and its location.
3. **Setup**: capacity, branding and logo. If you like, import your students from a CSV or Excel file now, or later from bulk import.
4. **Business**: visibility, price, terms, and your school's web address, for example `yourschool.balqalam.com`.
5. Accept the terms. The school is activated, with its default grades, departments, terms and roles ready to use.
6. Then open your school at its address and sign in. Add your people by [bulk import](/docs/support/bulk-student) from Excel or one by one, check the [academic setup](/docs/support/academics), look over the [timetable](/docs/support/timetable) Balqalam fills for you, set up [fees](/docs/support/fees), and hand out logins.

## Good to know

- Setup usually takes 10–30 minutes.
- Every school gets a full **three-month free trial**, with no fees and no commitment. After it, the first 100 students stay free forever.
- You can change the name, logo and most details later in **School → Configuration**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The web address is taken** — pick another one, for example add your city: `yourschool-khartoum`.
- **The setup stopped halfway** — click **Get Started** again and sign in with the same account. Your progress is kept.
- **The school address doesn't open** — check the spelling of the address, then try again after a few minutes.
