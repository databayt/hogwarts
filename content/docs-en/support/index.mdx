Each guide walks through one task the way it looks in the app, with a tutorial video and screenshots where we have recorded them. Can't find something? Ask the assistant in the corner of any page. It answers from these guides and attaches the matching video. You can also read the [common questions](/docs/support/faq) or email **contact@databayt.org**.
