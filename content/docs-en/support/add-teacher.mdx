## Steps

1. Open **Teachers** from the sidebar, then click the round **+** (Create) button.
2. **Documents**: upload the teacher's photo and CV if you have them.
3. **Basic information**: name and personal details.
4. **Expertise**: pick the subjects and sections they teach.
5. **Contact**: an email is required. Then fill in **Address** and **Employment** details, and click **Create**.
6. To give the teacher a login, open the row's actions in the teachers list and choose **Generate Credentials**.

## Good to know

- Bringing in your whole staff? Bulk-import teachers from one file instead.
