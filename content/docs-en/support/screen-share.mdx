## Steps

1. On Android, join the class **from the app** (from **Live** or the timetable). A phone browser can't share the screen or keep the class running in the background.
2. Tap **Share screen** inside the class and accept the permission prompt.
3. Open the book, video or site you want to show, then come back to the class whenever you like: the share keeps running.
4. Students hear the **video's sound** with the screen, and your voice too while you share.
5. To stop, tap **Stop sharing**. To draw or write, tap the **whiteboard** button.

## Good to know

- On a computer pick a browser **tab** and turn on tab audio so students hear the video.
- Screen sharing is not available on iPhone yet. Use a computer or an Android phone.
- Practice with a colleague before your class in the **test room**.
- On mobile data the app sends the screen at a lighter quality so it doesn't drown out the sound. Wi-Fi is better.

## Troubleshooting

- **The voice cuts out while sharing on mobile data**: update the app with the **Update** button (the new version sends the screen at a lighter quality) and prefer Wi-Fi. If it still cuts after updating, report the problem with the class name and time.
- **Students can't hear me while I share**: update the app, and make sure you joined from the app, not the browser.
- **Sharing doesn't work or has no button**: you are in the phone browser; open the class from the app. If it fails after updating, choose **Entire screen** instead of a single app.
- **The class closes when I open the book**: start sharing first, then open the book, and don't leave the app before sharing begins.
- **The whiteboard and the share don't work together**: only one shows at a time; stop the first, then start the second.
