## Steps

1. Open **Exams → Quick checks** and click **New assessment**.
2. Enter a title and choose the type: **Exit ticket**, **Poll**, **Warm-up** or **Check-in**, and a time limit.
3. Choose the grade, the section (or the whole grade) and the subject.
4. Tick the questions from the subject's question bank, then click **Create assessment**.
5. In a live class, open **Class tools** and launch it. Students answer in the room, and the results reach the gradebook.

## Good to know

- Questions come from the school's question bank for that subject. A new school's bank is empty: add questions under **Exams → Question bank** first.
- **Anonymous responses** hide who answered; **Show results to students** shows them the results afterwards.

## Troubleshooting

- **"This subject has no questions in the bank yet"**: add questions to the subject in **Question bank**, then come back.
- **The check doesn't show in the room**: it was created for another grade or subject than the class.
