## Steps

1. Open **Timetable** and click today's class while it is running, or up to 10 minutes before it starts. You can also click the class card at the top of your home page.
2. The class page opens with its picture, teacher and time. Click **Join**.
3. Allow the browser to use your camera and microphone if it asks. Students enter with the microphone off and the camera off.
4. In the class:
   - **Raise hand** to ask to speak. The teacher sees your place in line.
   - **Chat** for messages to the class, and **Questions** for questions the teacher answers.
   - The teacher may share the **Whiteboard**, slides or their screen. They fill the screen on their own.
5. Click **Leave** to leave. If the connection drops, the class says **Reconnecting…**; if that fails, click **Rejoin**.

## Good to know

- The room is open at the class's time, so go in whenever you arrive: you may get there before the teacher or after, and you never wait for anyone.
- If the teacher is not in yet, stay in the room: it tells you the teacher has not come in, and the teacher is notified that students are waiting. If the teacher is in before the students, you get a **"… is in the class now"** notification.
- Turn on **class alerts** once on each device: under the Join button and on your home page you are offered "Turn on class alerts to get a notification on this device before each class". Click **Turn on**. On iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first, then turn alerts on from the installed app.
- Parents can watch a child's class when the school allows it. They join as observers, with no camera, microphone or questions.
- Your attendance can be taken from the class: staying in the room counts, a short visit does not.
- A class you missed shows on the **Live** page under the classes you can catch up on, with its recording when the school records classes.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You are not enrolled in this class"**: the class belongs to another section. Check you opened your own class from your timetable.
- **No sound or picture**: click **Test my camera and connection** under the Join button, and check the browser has permission to use the camera and microphone.
- **No Join on the timetable**: the class is not online today, or it is more than 10 minutes away.
- **No class notifications**: click **Turn on** in the class alerts offer. If it says notifications are blocked, allow them for this site in your browser settings.
