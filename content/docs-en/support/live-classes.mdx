## Steps

1. Open **Timetable** and click today's class while it is running, or up to 10 minutes before it starts. You can also click the class card at the top of your home page.
2. The class page opens with its picture, teacher and time. Click **Join**.
3. Allow the browser to use your camera and microphone if it asks. Students enter with the microphone off and the camera off.
4. In the class:
   - **Raise hand** to ask to speak. The teacher sees your place in line.
   - **Chat** for messages to the class, and **Questions** for questions the teacher answers.
   - The teacher may share the **Whiteboard**, slides or their screen. They fill the screen on their own.
5. Click **Leave** to leave. If the connection drops, the class says **Reconnecting…**; if that fails, click **Rejoin**.

## Good to know

- The room is open at the class's time, so go in whenever you arrive: you may get there before the teacher or after, and you never wait for anyone.
- If the teacher is not in yet, stay in the room: it tells you the teacher has not come in, and the teacher is notified that students are waiting. If the teacher is in before the students, you get a **"… is in the class now"** notification.
- Turn on **class alerts** once on each device: under the Join button and on your home page you are offered "Turn on class alerts to get a notification on this device before each class". Click **Turn on**. On iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first, then turn alerts on from the installed app.
- Parents can watch a child's class when the school allows it. They join as observers, with no camera, microphone or questions.
- Your attendance can be taken from the class: staying in the room counts, a short visit does not.
- A class you missed shows on the **Live** page under the classes you can catch up on, with its recording when the school records classes.
- On an Android phone classes open in the **app** only; the page shows **Open the app** or **Get the app**. If you can't install it, **I couldn't install the app** lets you in through the browser for that day and tells the supervisor. iPhone and computers use the browser as usual. See [Installing the app](/docs/support/install-app).
- Class time is **school time**, and the join button appears when the class starts.

## Microphone or camera blocked

When you see **"The microphone is blocked"** or **"The camera is blocked"**, the browser is refusing access. The room shows a window with the steps for your own browser, and once you allow it the microphone or camera turns on **by itself, with no rejoin**. If you close the window, tap the red chip **"Microphone blocked — tap to fix"** at the top of the screen.

- **Chrome on Android**: tap the icon at the left of the address, then **Permissions**, then **Microphone** (or Camera), then **Allow**. No Allow option? Phone Settings, Apps, Chrome, Permissions, Microphone, Allow.
- **Samsung Internet**: menu ☰, Settings, **Sites and downloads**, **Site permissions**, Microphone, allow this site.
- **Safari on iPhone**: tap **aA** in the address bar, **Website Settings**, Microphone, **Allow**. Still blocked: iPhone Settings, Safari, Microphone, Ask or Allow.
- **Chrome on iPhone**: iPhone Settings, Chrome, turn on Microphone and Camera.
- **Chrome or Edge on a computer**: the lock icon at the left of the address, Microphone, **Allow**.
- **Firefox**: the permission icon next to the address, remove the block on the microphone.
- **Safari on Mac**: Safari menu, Settings, Websites, Microphone, Allow. Still blocked: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone, turn on your browser.
- **Inside WhatsApp or Facebook**: the microphone does not work there at all. Tap **Copy the link** and open it in Chrome, or open the class in the **app** on Android.

## Troubleshooting

- **I can't sign in**: type the 6-digit **username** and the password your school sent, not your personal email. Your first sign-in asks for a new password: type one and keep it. If it's refused, ask the school for new details.
- **"You are not enrolled in this class"**: the class belongs to another section. Check you opened your own class from your timetable.
- **No sound or picture**: click **Test my camera and connection** under the Join button, and check the browser has permission to use the camera and microphone.
- **No Join on the timetable**: the class is not online today, or it is more than 10 minutes away.
- **No class notifications**: click **Turn on** in the class alerts offer. If it says notifications are blocked, allow them for this site in your browser settings.
- **No microphone or camera**: open phone Settings ← the app ← **Permissions** and allow the microphone and camera, then use their buttons inside the class.
- **The microphone is red or "turns itself off"**: students join muted, so tap the **red microphone** at the bottom of the class to turn it white and speak. If it turns red again, a message says why: allow the microphone for the site from the lock icon next to the address, and do not open the class from inside WhatsApp or Facebook — open it in Chrome, or in the **app** on Android.
- **The teacher hasn't joined and I'm in the class**: after five minutes the class supervisor is alerted and covers it or books a [make-up class](/docs/support/makeup-class).
