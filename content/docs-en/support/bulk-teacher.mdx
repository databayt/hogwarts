## Steps

1. Open **School → Bulk**. This page is for admins only.
2. On the **Teachers** card, download the **Template** if you like, then choose your file: Excel, CSV and more, up to 10 MB and 5,000 rows.
3. **Match columns**: name, email, department and subjects are matched automatically. Check them.
4. **Review**: check that every row is **New**. Nothing is saved until you import.
5. Click **Import**. Teacher accounts are created. Then use **Download logins** or **Print login slips**.

## Good to know

- To remove an import, click **Undo** next to it in **Import history**. People who have already signed in are kept.

## Troubleshooting

- **The file is rejected** — use Excel, CSV or similar, up to 10 MB and 5,000 rows. Start from the **Template**.
- **Rows show Error** — usually a missing or repeated email. Every teacher needs their own email; fix it in the file and import again.
- **Rows show Skip** — those teachers already exist. Turn on **Update people who already exist** to write their changes.
- **Departments or subjects are missing** — turn on **Create missing sections and departments**, or check the spelling in your file.
- **The logins file has new passwords** — each download sets new passwords for accounts that haven't signed in yet. Hand out the latest file only.
