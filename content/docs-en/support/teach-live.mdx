## Steps

A live class works like the classroom: the room is there at the class's time on the timetable, and the teacher and the students each walk in when they arrive, in any order. Nobody "starts" the class and nobody waits for anyone.

1. Open **Timetable** and click **Join** on your class while it is running, or up to 10 minutes before. You can also go in from the green banner on your home page, or from the class notification.
2. You enter the room straight away. Some students may already be inside; that's normal.
3. Run the class:
   - **Raised hands** show in order. **Give the floor** brings a student to the stage.
   - Open the **Whiteboard**, share slides or your screen, and run a **Poll** or a quick check.
   - Answer the **Questions** and keep an eye on the **Chat**.
4. When the class is over, click **Leave**. The room closes on its own 10 minutes after the class's time.
5. After the class, choose **Set homework** to give the section homework straight away. Students are notified.

## Good to know

- If everyone leaves the room and comes back before the class's time is up, it is still the same class.
- Attendance is written on its own after the class's time, if your school has turned on **Auto-mark attendance**. Lateness counts from the bell, not from when you arrived. You can correct any student on the class page.
- You can dismiss the class early from the class page: **End class**, then **End for everyone?**. This is optional.
- A substitute teacher who has been confirmed for your period goes in instead of you.
- Students enter with the microphone and camera off; you can change that per class.

## Troubleshooting

- **No Join button on the timetable**: the class is more than 10 minutes away, your school is set to **In person**, or it isn't your period. Ask the admin.
- **A student can't hear you**: check your microphone in **More**, and ask them to click **Test my camera and connection**.
- **Attendance wasn't written**: automatic attendance is off for the school, or nobody on the roster joined.
