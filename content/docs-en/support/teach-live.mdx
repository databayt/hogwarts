## Steps

1. Open **Timetable** and click your class while it is running, or up to 10 minutes before. If no room exists yet, the click starts the class for you.
2. On the class page, click **Join**. You are the host; the class becomes live for your students.
3. Run the class:
   - **Raised hands** show in order. **Give the floor** brings a student to the stage.
   - Open the **Whiteboard**, share slides or your screen, and run a **Poll** or a quick check.
   - Answer the **Questions** and keep an eye on the **Chat**.
4. Click **End class** and confirm **End for everyone?** to close the room.
5. After the class, choose **Set homework** to give the section homework straight away. Students are notified.

## Good to know

- Attendance is written on its own when the class ends, if your school has turned on **Auto-mark attendance**. You can correct any student on the class page.
- A substitute teacher who has been confirmed for your period hosts the class instead of you.
- Students enter with the microphone and camera off; you can change that per class.

## Troubleshooting

- **The click on the timetable doesn't start the class**: your school is set to **In person**, or it isn't your period. Ask the admin.
- **A student can't hear you**: check your microphone in **More**, and ask them to click **Test my camera and connection**.
- **Attendance wasn't written**: automatic attendance is off for the school, or nobody on the roster joined.
