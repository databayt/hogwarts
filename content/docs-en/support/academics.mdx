## Steps

- **Timetable**: build a weekly schedule for each section by hand, or generate it automatically. Conflicts are checked for you, and you can export it to PDF.
- **Exams**: use the question bank, generate exam papers, run online exams with automatic marking, and issue certificates.
- **Grades**: produce report cards and transcripts, promote students, and design report-card templates.

## Good to know

- Students and parents see grades and exams in their own views.
- Teachers see the timetable, exams, attendance and grades.
