## Steps

1. Open **School → Configuration → Academic Structure** to set the school years, terms and grading.
2. Open **Subjects** from the sidebar to see and edit what each grade studies.
3. Open **Classrooms** to manage the sections and rooms.
4. Then follow the guide you need: the [Timetable](/docs/support/timetable), [Exams](/docs/support/exams), or [Grades and report cards](/docs/support/grades).

## Good to know

- A new school starts with its default grades, departments and terms ready, so you can begin right away.
- Report cards, transcripts and the timetable all work by term, so check the current term first.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No academic terms yet"** — add the year and its terms in **Academic Structure**.
- **A subject is missing from the timetable** — add it to the grade in **Subjects**, then generate the timetable again with **Merge**.
