## Steps

1. Click the bell at the top of the page to open **Notifications**, then the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Push notifications on this device**, click **Turn on** and allow notifications when the browser asks.
3. On iPhone, first add the app to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), then turn notifications on from the installed app.
4. Choose which kinds of notification you want by email and by push.

## Good to know

- You're notified when a class you belong to is starting soon and when it starts (with a link straight into the class), when homework is set, and about absences, announcements and messages.
- The bell at the top of the dashboard shows every notification, even with push off.
- Turn notifications on separately on each device you use.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Notifications are blocked for this site"**: allow notifications for the site in your browser settings, then click **Turn on** again.
- **"This browser does not support push notifications"**: use a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, or install the app on your phone.
- **A class reminder came late or not at all**: reminders are sent shortly before each class; check push is on for this device.
