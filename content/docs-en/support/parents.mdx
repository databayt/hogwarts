## Steps

1. Add the student. The wizard saves the parent as a contact.
2. Give the parent a login: use **Generate Credentials**, import guardians in bulk, or link them with an access code.
3. The parent signs in and opens the **parent portal**.

## Good to know

- The parent portal shows their children, fees, announcements, events and messages, plus the children's attendance, grades and exams.
- Parents are notified automatically when their child is absent, and can submit an excuse.
