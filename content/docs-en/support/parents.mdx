## Steps

1. **Add the student.** The wizard saves the parent as a contact.
2. **Give the parent a login.** In **Parents**, open the parent's row and click **Generate Credentials**. Share the login code and PIN by WhatsApp, SMS or email, or copy them. Importing guardians in bulk works too.
3. **Link a parent to another child.** In **Students**, open the child's row and click **Link Parent** to get an access code you can print. Then in **Parents**, open the parent's row, click **Link Child**, enter the **Access Code**, click **Validate Code**, choose the relationship and click **Confirm Link**.
4. **The parent signs in** at the school's address and opens **Parent portal** from the sidebar.
5. They pick a child and use the tabs: **Overview**, **Grades**, **Report cards**, **Attendance**, **Timetable** and **Assignments**.

## Good to know

- Parents also see announcements, events and messages, and their children's fees under **Finance**.
- When a child is marked absent, the parent is notified. They can click **Submit Excuse** on the child's **Attendance** tab, with a reason and an optional attachment. A teacher then reviews it.
- On first sign-in, parents choose their own password.
- When your child's class is online today it shows at the top of your home page. You can watch it when the school allows guardians to, as an observer with no camera or microphone.

## Troubleshooting

- **A parent sees no children**: the login isn't linked yet. Use **Link Parent** and **Link Child** from step 3.
- **The access code is rejected**: codes expire. Generate a new one with **Link Parent**.
- **A parent can't sign in**: see [Signing in and passwords](/docs/support/login). The school office can reset their password with **Generate Credentials**.
- **A parent with two children only sees one**: link the second child with its own access code.
