## Steps

**Student: hand in**

1. Open **My assignments**. New homework is listed first, with its due date.
2. Write your answer, and click **Attach files** to add photos of your work, a PDF, a document or a voice note (up to 25MB each, 10 files).
3. Click **Submit**. You can resubmit until the teacher marks it; resubmitting replaces what you handed in.

**Teacher: set and mark**

1. Open **Assignments → Create assignment**, choose the grade, the section or the whole grade, the subject and the type, then the points and due date. Finishing publishes it and notifies the students.
2. Open the assignment and the **Submissions** tab. You see who handed in (for example "12 of 25 handed in"), their text and their files.
3. Enter the **Score** and **Feedback** for each hand-in and click **Save**.

## Good to know

- After a live class, **Set homework** on the class page gives the section homework in one step.
- Students get a reminder the day before the due date. Work handed in after the due date is marked **Late**.
- Parents see their child's homework and marks in the parent portal.

## Troubleshooting

- **A student doesn't see the homework**: check it was set for their grade or section, and that the assignment wizard was finished.
- **"Files need a connection to hand in"**: files can't be saved offline. Hand in when you're back online; text alone can be saved offline.
- **An upload fails**: check the file type and size (25MB at most), then try again.
