## Steps

1. Open **Students** from the sidebar, then click the round **+** (Create) button.
2. **Documents**: upload the student's photo and files, such as an ID or transcript. Some fields fill in from them automatically.
3. **Personal details**: the student's name, and the guardian's details in the **Father** and **Mother** tabs.
4. **Address**: where the student lives.
5. **Academic**: grade, stream and section. Click **Create**.
6. The student's **login** appears: a username and password. Copy it, or send it by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

## Good to know

- Only the student's name and one parent are required. You can fill in the rest later.
- The wizard saves the parent as a contact. To give the parent their own login, use **Generate Credentials** on the student, or import guardians in bulk.
- Adding a whole class? Bulk import is faster.
