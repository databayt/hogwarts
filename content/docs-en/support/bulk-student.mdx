## Steps

1. Open **School → Bulk**. This page is for admins only.
2. On the **Students** card, download the **Template** if you want a ready-made file. Then choose your file: Excel, CSV, JSON, Word or text, up to 10 MB and 5,000 rows.
3. **Match columns**: your columns are matched automatically, in Arabic or English. Check them, and fix any that are wrong.
4. **Review**: every row shows as **New**, **Update**, **Skip** or **Error**. Nothing is saved until you import.
5. Click **Import**. Then use **Download logins** for a file, or **Print login slips** for slips with QR codes.

## Good to know

- Each user chooses their own password the first time they sign in.
- Imported the wrong file? Find it in **Import history** and click **Undo**. This removes the people that import created, except anyone who has already signed in.

## Troubleshooting

- **The file is rejected** — use Excel, CSV, JSON, Word or text, up to 10 MB and 5,000 rows. Starting from the **Template** avoids most problems.
- **"Choose a column for: …"** — a required field has no column. Pick the right column in **Match columns**, or add it to your file.
- **"This looks like a … file"** — you picked the wrong card, for example a teachers file on **Students**. Click **Import as …** to switch.
- **Rows show Skip** — those students already exist. Turn on **Update people who already exist** to write their changes instead.
- **Rows show Error** — open the row to see why, fix it in your file, and import again. Rows that went in are not duplicated.
- **The import stopped halfway** — find it in **Import history** and click **Resume**.
