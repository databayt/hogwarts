## Steps

1. Open the link the school sent you: your **school's own address**, such as `yourschool.balqalam.com/en/login`.
2. In the first box, **Email or username**, type your **6-digit username** exactly as you received it, not your name.
3. In the second box type your **Password** (4 digits) and click **Login**.
4. The first time, Balqalam asks you to **Choose your own password**: type it twice, click **Save and continue**, and keep it somewhere safe.
5. Your **Dashboard** opens. If you see the school's public home page instead, open the **☰** menu and tap **Platform**.
6. Click **Timetable** to see your periods for the week: subject, class, day and time.
7. When a period is due, click it in the **Timetable** to start the live class and let your students in.

## Good to know

- Your username and password are yours alone, so don't share them.
- Your subjects, classes and timetable are set by the **school office**. If something looks wrong, tell them and they will fix it.
- Install the platform on your phone from the browser to open it in one tap. [Mobile app](/docs/support/mobile)

## Troubleshooting

- **"Account not found"**: type the six digits from your message in the first box, not your name or phone number. If it keeps happening, your account hasn't been created yet, so ask the school office to **Generate Credentials**.
- **I see "Start a new application" or "Start from scratch"**: that is the application form for new students, reached from **Don't have an account?**. Teachers don't apply, so go back to the login page.
- **"Access Denied"**: you are signed in with an account that doesn't belong to this school. Use **Logout** in the menu, then sign in with the code your school gave you.
- **My timetable is empty or a subject is missing**: the school hasn't assigned it or entered the timetable yet. Send them your subjects, classes and timetable.
- **The box looks filled but says "required"**: the browser is suggesting a saved email without typing it. Tap the box and type your username yourself.
