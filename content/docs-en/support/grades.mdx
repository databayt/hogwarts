## Steps

1. Open **Grades** from the sidebar. **Gradebook** holds every mark from exams and homework; click **Add Grade** to enter one by hand.
2. At the end of the term, open **Report cards**, pick the **Term**, and click **Generate Report Cards**. Choose the whole school, a grade or a section.
3. Review the drafts (**Draft**), then click **Publish**, or **Publish all**. Students and guardians are notified.
4. To print on the school's own design, use **Download all (school template)**. Share one card by **Copy share link** or **Send on WhatsApp**.
5. Issue a transcript from **Transcripts**. It carries a code anyone can check online.
6. At year end, open **Promotion**: set the rules per grade, review who meets them, change any decision, then approve.

## Good to know

- Marks entered or published in **Exams** land in the gradebook by themselves. Nothing is typed twice.
- Each report card shows subject marks, attendance and the student's rank.
- Report cards stay drafts until you publish them. Parents see them from their child's page.
- Upload your report-card Word template once in **Templates**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Skipped — no grades to aggregate"** — those students have no marks in that term. Publish the exam results or add grades, then generate again.
- **"No academic terms yet"** — add the school year and terms in **School → Configuration → Academic Structure** first.
- **A parent can't see the report card** — it is still a draft. Click **Publish**, and make sure the parent is linked to the student.
- **A mark on the card is wrong** — fix it in the gradebook or the exam, then generate the card again before publishing.
- **A shared link stopped opening** — someone clicked **Stop sharing**. Copy a new link.
