## Steps

1. **Android (Chrome)**: open your school's address and tap **Continue** to install.
2. **iPhone (Safari)**: open your school's address, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Good to know

- Balqalam installs as an app, with your school's name and colours.
- Quick Attendance and Lumos lessons keep working offline and sync later. Other pages need a connection.
