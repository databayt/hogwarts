## Steps

1. On your phone, open your school's address (for example `yourschool.balqalam.com`) and sign in.
2. A sheet appears: **Add balqalam to your Home Screen**. Tap **Continue**.
3. **Android (Chrome)**: confirm the install prompt. The app icon is added to your home screen.
4. **iPhone (Safari)**: in the share sheet, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
5. Open Balqalam from its icon. It opens full screen like a normal app.

## Good to know

- There's no App Store or Play Store download. The app installs from the website, with your school's name and colours.
- Offline, **Quick Attendance**, Lumos lessons, quizzes and assignment submissions are saved on the phone and sent when the connection returns.
- Pages you've already opened can be reopened offline. On a shared phone they're cleared when you sign out.
- Turn on push notifications to hear about classes starting and new homework, even with the app closed. See [Notifications](/docs/support/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

- **The install sheet doesn't appear**: it only shows on phones, not on a computer or once the app is installed, and it stays hidden for 14 days after you close it. Install from the browser menu instead: Chrome **⋮ → Install app**, or Safari **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- **iPhone has no "Add to Home Screen"**: open the address in **Safari**, not in WhatsApp's built-in browser or another app.
- **"Attendance saved on this device" won't go away**: connect to the internet and open the app. It syncs on its own.
- **A page says it can't load offline**: you haven't opened that page on this phone yet. Open it once while you're online.
