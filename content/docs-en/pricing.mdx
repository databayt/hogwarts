## Why it pays

One public price makes every quote fast and every deal comparable. A per-student price grows with the school: a small school pays little, and when a school grows, so do we.

## The price

| Students | A year | First payment (50%) | Then 4 payments (12.5% each) |
| -------- | ------ | ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 50       | $320   | $160                | $40                          |
| 100      | $640   | $320                | $80                          |
| 250      | $1,600 | $800                | $200                         |
| 500      | $3,200 | $1,600              | $400                         |

- No free plan, no bands, no setup fee.
- Over 1,000 students: the same rate, with its own contract.

No discounts protects the price for everyone. A discount given once becomes the price the next school asks for.

## How a school pays

- The free month ends, then the school pays 50%.
- Then 12.5% at months 3, 6, 9 and 12.
- Each payment counts the students on that day.
- In Sudan the price is in US dollars, paid in Sudanese pounds at the bank rate on the payment day, by Bankak.

## How to quote

1. Ask how many students the school has.
2. Multiply by $6.40, or use the calculator at balqalam.com/pricing.
3. Send the total and the payment plan in one message.

## When this happens

- **"It's too expensive"** → ask what they spend today, in money and in staff hours, on fees, attendance and the timetable. Then offer the free month to judge it. Never cut the price.
- **"Can you give us a discount?"** → "Our price is the same for every school. The first month is free, so you can judge it first."
- **The school has more than 1,000 students** → the same rate, its own contract. Send the deal to Abdout.
- **A school outside Sudan asks** → quote 24 SAR per student a year, and send the deal to Abdout for how it pays.
