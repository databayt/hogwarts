**We grow with partners, not alone.**

## Why it pays

A partner who already knows school owners is the cheapest sales we have: we pay only when a school pays. On a 300-student school, a partner earns about $960 of the first year, and we keep the rest of that year and every year after.

## Kinds of partners

| Partner                          | They give                            | They get                              |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Founding schools                 | Feedback and a story we can share    | Our closest support                   |
| Sales partners                   | Schools they bring                   | A commission (below)                  |
| Sponsors                         | Free cloud and AI credits            | Their name on our site                |
| Institutions (NGOs, ministries)  | Reach to many schools                | One platform for all their schools    |

## Sales-partner commission

- 50% of a school's first-year payments, for each of your first five schools.
- 30% from the sixth school on.
- First year only, paid within 30 days of the school paying us, on what the school actually paid.
- It starts when the school signs and pays — not on a promise.

Paying on real payments, never on promises, keeps the commission fair for both sides.

## When this happens

- **A partner brings a school** → log the school in the CRM with the partner's name before the first meeting. That is what makes the commission count.
- **A partner asks for exclusivity or a different rate** → promise nothing, and send it to Abdout.
- **A sponsor offers credits** → post it in the group with its deadline (see [Fundraising](/docs/fundraising)).
- **An NGO or a ministry wants many schools** → treat it like a school group (Sales, "Groups first"), and tell Abdout the same day.

## What you do

- **Mutaz** finds and signs the sales partners.
- Before you promise a partner anything, send it to Abdout.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** half the first year is generous for a company with no salaries yet.
- **What would change it:** after five partner schools, compare how many stay in year two with schools we found ourselves. If partner schools stay as well or better, the rate stays.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: partners — your reason» in the group.
