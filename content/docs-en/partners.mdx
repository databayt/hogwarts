**We grow with partners, not alone.**

## Kinds of partners

| Partner                          | They give                            | They get                              |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Founding schools                 | Feedback and a story we can share    | Our closest support                   |
| Sales partners                   | Schools they bring                   | A commission (below)                  |
| Sponsors                         | Free cloud and AI credits            | Their name on our site                |
| Institutions (NGOs, ministries)  | Reach to many schools                | One platform for all their schools    |

## Sales-partner commission

- 50% of a school's first-year payments, for each of your first five schools.
- 30% from the sixth school on.
- First year only, paid within 30 days of the school paying us, on what the school actually paid.
- It starts when the school signs and pays — not on a promise.

## What you do

- **Mutaz** finds and signs the sales partners.
- Before you promise a partner anything, send it to Abdout.
