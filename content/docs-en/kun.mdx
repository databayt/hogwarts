**Kun («كن», "Be") is how a small team works like a big one: Claude, working inside our tools.**

## Why it pays

Kun is why a team with no salaries can run like a company. It writes the code, the plans and the drafts, so people spend their hours with schools. It costs a share of the ~$500 a month on [Monetize](/docs/monetize), not a salary.

## What Kun does

- Builds and fixes Hogwarts.
- Writes these pages and keeps them current.
- Drafts the weekly plan and the messages to the team.
- Reads the problems schools report from the app.
- Watches the servers and the costs.

## What Kun never does alone

- Send a message to the team or to a school.
- Spend money, sign anything, or change a price.
- Delete a school's data.

Each of these waits for Abdout's yes. People tend to trust automated advice too much, so a person signs off on anything that leaves the company.

## How you use it

- In the app: «Report an issue». It reaches Kun and the team.
- In the group: ask your question. It reaches Kun through Abdout.
- Messages from Kun start with «🧭 مكتب التشغيل».

## When this happens

- **A Kun draft is wrong** → say so in the group, or write «اعتراض: kun — your reason». A draft is only a draft until Abdout approves it.
- **You want Kun to do something** → ask in the group. It reaches Kun through Abdout.
- **The app breaks** → «Report an issue», with a screenshot. It reaches Kun and the team.
- **A message claims to come from Kun without «🧭 مكتب التشغيل»** → it is not from the office. Ignore it.

## What you do

- Report problems from the app, with a screenshot.
- Read the Sunday plan: Kun writes it, Abdout approves it.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** an AI that drafts everything can make a team passive, or be confidently wrong.
- **What would change it:** every Kun mistake reported in a week is counted at Thursday's review. Two of the same kind change how Kun works.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: kun — your reason» in the group.
