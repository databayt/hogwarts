## Why it pays

A task "for the team" waits for everyone, so nobody does it. A task with one owner and a day gets done. Owning a part is also how each of us shares in what the company earns: your work is recorded under your name (see [Shared economy](/docs/shared-economy)). This page is the only place that says who owns what, so when a role changes, only this page changes.

## Who decides

- **The owner decides** anything easy to undo: a message, a post, a demo time, a task's order.
- **The CEO office decides** anything hard to undo: money, a price, a discount, a contract, hiring, anything sent in the company's name to an investor or a government. While the CEO is on leave, Huwaida holds it as vice president.
- **Thursday's review** settles disagreements, and changes the page the same day. Every one of us has a voice there.

## How we work: research first

Nothing on these pages is a guess. Before we start something new — a price, a sales step, a rule — we follow the same sequence:

1. **Search.** What does research say? What do the people who did it well do?
2. **Take the best practice**, and the inspiration, that fits a small team and Sudanese schools.
3. **Write it down** as a page here, with its sources under References.
4. **Try it** for one sprint.
5. **Thursday's review** keeps it or changes it, and the page changes the same day.

Every Team page ends with References: the books, studies and practitioners behind it. When someone asks "why do we sell this way?" or "why this price?", the answer is there. Found a better source? Bring it to Thursday's review.

## When this happens

- **Two people think they own the same task** → whoever took it first owns it. The other helps.
- **A task has no owner** → take it, or raise it on Tuesday.
- **You will be away** → say so in the group, and hand each open task to someone by name.
- **Your role changes** → tell Huwaida, and this page changes the same week.

## What you do

Know your row. Before you ask the group, check whose row the question belongs to.

## References

Where this page's way of working comes from:

- Latané, Williams & Harkins (1979), ["Many hands make light the work"](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.37.6.822), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* — people put in less effort when a task belongs to a group. Hence one owner per task.
- Bezos (2016), [2015 Letter to Shareholders](https://s2.q4cdn.com/299287126/files/doc_financials/annual/2015-Letter-to-Shareholders.PDF), Amazon — "one-way" and "two-way door" decisions: decide the reversible ones fast, and slow down only for those you can't undo. Our "easy to undo?" rule.
- Pfeffer & Sutton (2006), ["Evidence-Based Management"](https://hbr.org/2006/01/evidence-based-management), *Harvard Business Review* — decide from the best evidence available, not from habit or from what others do. The basis of "research first".
- The Deming Institute, [The PDSA cycle](https://deming.org/explore/pdsa/) — plan, do, study, act: try a change small, study what happened, then keep it or change it. Our sprint, then Thursday's review.
