## Why it pays

A task "for the team" waits for everyone, so nobody does it. A task with one owner and a day gets done. Owning a part is also how each of us shares in what the company earns: your work is recorded under your name (see [Shared economy](/docs/shared-economy)). This page is the only place that says who owns what, so when a role changes, only this page changes.

## Who decides

- **The owner decides** anything easy to undo: a message, a post, a demo time, a task's order.
- **The CEO office decides** anything hard to undo: money, a price, a discount, a contract, hiring, anything sent in the company's name to an investor or a government. While the CEO is on leave, Huwaida holds it as vice president.
- **Thursday's review** settles disagreements, and changes the page the same day. Every one of us has a voice there.

## When this happens

- **Two people think they own the same task** → whoever took it first owns it. The other helps.
- **A task has no owner** → take it, or raise it on Tuesday.
- **You will be away** → say so in the group, and hand each open task to someone by name.
- **Your role changes** → tell Huwaida, and this page changes the same week.

## What you do

Know your row. Before you ask the group, check whose row the question belongs to.
