## Why it pays

A task "for the team" waits for everyone, so nobody does it. A task with one owner and a day gets done. This page is the only place that says who owns what. The other pages point here, so when a role changes, only this page changes.

## Who decides

- **The owner decides** anything easy to undo: a message, a post, a demo time, a task's order.
- **Abdout's yes first** for anything hard to undo: money, a price, a discount, a contract, hiring, anything sent in the company's name to an investor or a government.
- **Thursday's review** settles arguments («اعتراض:»), and changes the page the same day.

## When this happens

- **Two people think they own the same task** → whoever wrote «أنا» first owns it. The other helps.
- **A task has no owner** → take it with «أنا», or raise it on Tuesday.
- **You will be away** → say so in the group, and hand each open task to someone by name.
- **Your role changes** → tell Abdout, and this page changes the same week.

## What you do

Know your row. Before you ask the group, check whose row the question belongs to.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** in a team of nine, fixed rows can stop people helping where help is needed most.
- **What would change it:** a department with open tasks for two weeks while others have none. Then Thursday's review moves people, and this page changes.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: team — your reason» in the group.
