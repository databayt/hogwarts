Annual SaaS subscription at one flat per-student rate: **24 SAR per student per year, with every school's first 100 students free forever**. Designed to scale from a 50-student private school to a national network of public schools. Live in production at [balqalam.com](https://balqalam.com); piloted with [King Fahad Schools](https://kingfahd.balqalam.com) — the founding member of the [MENA-10 free pilot cohort](/docs/pilot). For commercial process see [Sales](/docs/sales); for the pilot motion see [Pilot](/docs/pilot); for the unified go-to-market see [GTM](/docs/gtm).

## Subscription tiers

| Tier           | Price                                              | Best for                                            | Limits                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **FREE**       | 0 SAR, forever                                     | Small schools, evaluation                           | Up to 100 students and 10 teachers, 1 GB storage, community support, core features                                                                                 |
| **PRO**        | 24 SAR/student/year beyond the first 100 (≈ $6.40) | Growing schools                                     | Unlimited students and teachers, hosting, support by WhatsApp and email (Sunday–Thursday), updates, custom branding, advanced analytics, parent push notifications |
| **ENTERPRISE** | 24 SAR/student/year — the same rate                | Over 1,000 students, networks, government contracts | Dedicated contract, dedicated AM, 99.9% SLA, SSO (SAML/OAuth), white-label, unlimited storage and API, custom integrations                                         |

**Annual price = (students − 100) × 24 SAR.** No bands and no jumps: the price moves by exactly 24 SAR per student (≈ 2 SAR a month). Every school also gets a **full 3-month free trial**.

| Students | Billable | Annual price        | Per month (equivalent) |
| -------- | -------- | ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 100      | 0        | 0 SAR (free)        | 0 SAR                  |
| 150      | 50       | 1,200 SAR ($320)    | 100 SAR ($27)          |
| 250      | 150      | 3,600 SAR ($960)    | 300 SAR ($80)          |
| 300      | 200      | 4,800 SAR ($1,280)  | 400 SAR ($107)         |
| 500      | 400      | 9,600 SAR ($2,560)  | 800 SAR ($213)         |
| 1,000    | 900      | 21,600 SAR ($5,760) | 1,800 SAR ($480)       |

Prices exclude VAT (15% in Saudi Arabia). They are quoted in SAR; the [pricing page](https://balqalam.com/en/pricing) also shows USD, SDG (Sudanese pound) and EGP at the live daily exchange rate. At the time of writing, 24 SAR ≈ 6.40 USD ≈ 3,265 SDG (official rate) ≈ 335 EGP. The single source for every number on the site is `src/components/saas-marketing/pricing/rates.ts`.

### Why 24 SAR per student

The anchor is **6,000 SAR a year for a 250-student school**. Spread evenly, that is 24 SAR per student per year. Flat bands made a 100-student school pay 60 SAR a head; a flat per-student weight instead makes the bill grow in exact proportion to enrolment, one student at a time.

| Benchmark                     | Per student per year        |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Classe365                     | 24–60 SAR                   |
| Europe, off-the-shelf systems | ~20–65 SAR                  |
| India                         | ~2–27 SAR                   |
| **Balqalam**                  | **24 SAR** (first 100 free) |

24 SAR sits mid-market: at the floor of Classe365, the low end of Europe and the top of India's range. Infrastructure costs about **2.4–3.5% of the fee** without video. **Video LMS bandwidth is the main cost risk** — watch it on any school that leans on recorded lessons.

## Billing and payment

One annual price. There is no monthly plan, no monthly minimum, no monthly/yearly discount and no metered billing.

| Instalment | When       | Share | 250 students | 500 students |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----- | ------------ | ------------ |
| 1          | At signing | 50%   | 1,800 SAR    | 4,800 SAR    |
| 2          | Month 3    | 12.5% | 450 SAR      | 1,200 SAR    |
| 3          | Month 6    | 12.5% | 450 SAR      | 1,200 SAR    |
| 4          | Month 9    | 12.5% | 450 SAR      | 1,200 SAR    |
| 5          | Month 12   | 12.5% | 450 SAR      | 1,200 SAR    |

Multi-year terms are negotiated for government contracts.

**Payment methods**: bank transfer (schools, enterprise and government), Stripe for card payments (no metered subscriptions), and regional gateways (bankak, tap, mobile money) for MEA.

## Unit economics

### 500-student school (PRO)

| Metric                    | Value                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Annual revenue            | 9,600 SAR ($2,560) — (500 − 100) × 24 SAR                          |
| Monthly equivalent        | 800 SAR ($213)                                                     |
| Cash at signing           | 4,800 SAR ($1,280), then 1,200 SAR ($320) at months 3, 6, 9 and 12 |
| Customer acquisition cost | ~$500                                                              |
| Payback period            | At signing — the 50% upfront covers CAC                            |
| Gross margin              | 80%+ (infrastructure ≈ 2.4–3.5% of the fee without video)          |

### 5,000-student network (ENTERPRISE)

| Metric                    | Value                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annual revenue            | 117,600 SAR ($31,360) — (5,000 − 100) × 24 SAR, priced as one contract  |
| Monthly equivalent        | 9,800 SAR ($2,613)                                                      |
| Cash at signing           | 58,800 SAR ($15,680), then 14,700 SAR ($3,920) at months 3, 6, 9 and 12 |
| Customer acquisition cost | ~$3,000                                                                 |
| Payback period            | At signing                                                              |
| Gross margin              | 85%+                                                                    |

## Growth strategy

| Phase                 | Plan                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0. Free pilot cohort  | [MENA-10](/docs/pilot) — 10 free pilots, 3 months, feedback velocity → product polish |
| 1. Freemium           | FREE tier for ≤100 students; convert as they grow                                     |
| 2. Land + expand      | Pilot → 30%-off PRO Year 1, demonstrate ROI, propose network-wide ENTERPRISE          |
| 3. Government         | RFPs, custom compliance, multi-year volume contracts                                  |
| 4. Regional expansion | French / Spanish / Hindi / Mandarin locales; regional certifications                  |

See [GTM](/docs/gtm) for phase-gate logic — Phase 1 land-and-expand starts only after the cohort hits its conversion benchmarks. Free-pilot economics are intentional: ~$40 / pilot / month in infrastructure cost (Vercel + Neon + email), recovered if any single pilot converts at PRO 30%-off pricing.

## Cost structure

| Category      | Service                                                                       |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hosting       | Vercel (serverless), Neon (PostgreSQL serverless)                             |
| Storage / CDN | AWS S3 + CloudFront (`cdn.databayt.org`)                                      |
| Email / SMS   | Resend, Twilio                                                                |
| Payments      | Stripe (2.9% + $0.30/transaction), regional gateways                          |
| Development   | Core team + community contributors via [shared economy](/docs/shared-economy) |
| Compliance    | Annual security audit, certifications                                         |

### Target margins

- **Gross margin**: 80%+ (cloud-native efficiency)
- **Operating margin**: 20%+ at scale
- **LTV:CAC ratio**: 10:1+

## Marketplace and partnerships

A future integration marketplace (70/30 partner/platform split) and reseller network are on the roadmap; not active today.

### Template store (future)

A planned add-on revenue lumos: country-specific curriculum templates (Sudan curriculum, Saudi National Curriculum, IB MENA, Cambridge MENA, Azhar curriculum) sold to schools that adopt Balqalam. Pricing range $5–$50 per template with subscription bundles. Activation trigger: ≥3 pilots ship with a custom curriculum we can productize. Not active in 2026 — see [GTM](/docs/gtm) Phase 3.

## Equity and revenue sharing

All employees receive equity (4-year vest, 1-year cliff). Open-source contributors earn through the [shared economy](/docs/shared-economy) framework.
