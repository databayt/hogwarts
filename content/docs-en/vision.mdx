## Why it pays

The vision is an order of steps, not a wish list. Each step is paid for by the one before it, so we never spend money we have not earned. Today that means one thing: Sudanese schools paying $6.40 per student a year, until about 940 paying students (roughly ten schools) cover our costs.

## Now: Sudanese schools

- Our first school starts teaching on Hogwarts this month.
- Two to four more Sudanese schools are close.
- Most of our schools follow the Sudanese curriculum, so we built it first: grades 1 to 12, 136 subjects, with their books inside.

Winning one market completely beats being thin in five. Principals talk to principals, and a school trusts a system the school down the street already runs on.

## The steps

| Step                                                    | We open it when                       |

| 1. Sudanese schools, Sudanese curriculum                | Now                                   |
| 2. Other curricula (British and American are started)   | Sales brings a school that needs one  |
| 3. Institutes, courses, colleges, universities          | 10 schools pay us                     |
| 4. More countries, regions and languages                | The step before pays for itself       |

## Why tiny steps

- Each step must pay before the next one starts.
- A school from another country is welcome today: we finish its curriculum, then open that lane for everyone.
- We risk only what we can afford to lose. A new step is opened by a paying school asking for it, not by a guess about a market.

## When this happens

- **A school from another country or curriculum wants to join** → say yes, open the deal in the CRM, and tell Abdout the same day. Its curriculum becomes step 2 for everyone.
- **Someone proposes a new market, product or country** → ask which paying school is asking for it. None yet? Write it down under step 3 or 4, and keep selling step 1.
- **A school asks "why not universities, too?"** → step 3 opens at ten paying schools. The fastest way there is the next school.

## What you do

- Bring schools. Every paying school brings step 3 closer.
