## Why it pays

A sprint is two weeks with three goals, and every goal is a school that pays or signs. A short sprint with a fixed end makes a slip visible in days, not months. Our first payment, on 19 December, is three sprints away.

## The goals

1. **Al-Ibda** becomes a paying school: contract signed by 20 November, first payment 19 December.
2. **King Fahad** closes: their new year and branches on Hogwarts.
3. **Bakri's school in Port Sudan**: a first meeting, in person.

## Tasks by department

| Department | Tasks |
| ---------- | ----- |
| Business   | Al-Ibda contract signed · King Fahad offer for the new year and branches · first invoice ready for 19 December |
| Sales      | **Bakri:** ask Mutaz about the school · schedule the meeting (after 8 pm) · meet him in Port Sudan · follow up and close · **King Fahad:** schedule the demo · close · every deal in the CRM with its next date |
| Marketing  | Al-Ibda's first teaching week as a story (with permission) · posts in school-owner groups · the offer message |
| Tech       | Al-Ibda's first week runs clean: logins, live classes, timetable · King Fahad's new year set up · the free month in the app |

## How we decide

- Each department discusses its tasks in the group: business, sales, marketing, tech.
- Take a task by saying so in the group. Stuck, or disagree with a goal? Say that too, in your own words.
- Thursday's review checks every task.

A task with an owner and a day gets done. A task "for the team" waits for everyone, so nobody does it.

## The next sprints

- **Sprint 2 (22 November – 5 December):** two schools in their free month.
- **Sprint 3 (6–19 December):** first money — 19 December.
- **Sprint 4 (20 December – 2 January):** three paying schools.

## When this happens

- **You take a task** → say so in the group, with the day you will finish it.
- **A task will slip** → say so the day you know, with the new date. Never wait for Thursday.
- **You finish early** → take the next open task in your department, or help sales: it is the department closest to money.
- **A school says yes outside the plan** → put it in the CRM today and tell the group. It becomes a task in this sprint.

## What you do

- Take at least one task from your department each week, and say so in the group.
- On Thursday, bring three things: done, not done, and what you need.
