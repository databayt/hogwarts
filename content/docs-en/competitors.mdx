Hogwarts is an open-source, multi-tenant K-12 platform shipping in production at [ed.databayt.org](https://ed.databayt.org). Where the established SIS vendors target large North-American districts with closed, English-first stacks, Hogwarts is Arabic-first, RTL-native, subdomain-isolated per school, and priced for emerging-market budgets.

This page maps three distinct competitive sets — **global SIS incumbents** (the feature baseline), **MENA regional players** (who we actually meet in a deal), and **open-source peers** (the closest architectural analogues) — plus the assessment tools that shaped the exams module. The [battle-card section](#why-mena-schools-choose-hogwarts) at the bottom is the prose for proposals and discovery calls.

**Two trends worth naming in a sales call:**

- **Security is now a switching trigger, not a checkbox.** PowerSchool disclosed a breach in January 2025 exposing data on ~60M students and ~10M teachers; North Carolina districts have since migrated to Infinite Campus, citing security alongside features. Litigation reached the private-equity owner. Every school board in 2026 asks where the data lives — which is exactly the question open source and self-hosting answer well.
- **AI moved from add-on to differentiator.** Predictive analytics and workflow automation are now embedded in the major platforms rather than sold as modules. Our AI-assisted admission and auto-marking are table stakes going forward, not a moat on their own.

**What the regional set tells us:**

- **Arabic-first is real competition here, not a gap.** Madrasati, Noon, Edraak, and Alef all render RTL correctly and design Arabic-first. Our Arabic advantage holds against the _global_ incumbents and against Odoo/Edusys-style ports — it does not hold automatically against Classera or Alef. Lead with architecture and price in those rooms, not with "we support Arabic."
- **The regional leaders are funded and consolidating.** Classera's $40M Series A and its ERP acquisition mean the mid-market is being rolled up. Speed of onboarding and price for a single small school is where a solo school still gets a worse deal from them than from us.
- **Free national platforms set the floor.** In Saudi, "Madrasati is free" is the first objection. The answer is administration — fees, payroll, transport, admissions — which national learning platforms do not do.

## Open-source peers

Architecturally the closest set: schools can read the code, self-host, and leave. This is the group we are benchmarked against by technical buyers and by the community.

| Project                                         | Stack            | Note                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Gibbon](https://gibbonedu.org/)                | PHP              | Started 2010 (Hong Kong); fully free, no paid add-ons — the purest OSS option |
| [RosarioSIS](https://www.rosariosis.org/)       | PHP + PostgreSQL | Modular, lightweight, paid add-on modules                                     |
| [openSIS](https://www.opensis.com/)             | PHP              | Community edition + commercial tiers; K-12 and higher ed                      |
| [Fedena](https://www.fedena.com/)               | Ruby on Rails    | Open core, 22 modules, global install base                                    |
| [OpenEduCat](https://openeducat.org/)           | Odoo / Python    | ERP-shaped; inherits the Odoo module ecosystem                                |
| [Frappe Education](https://frappe.io/education) | Frappe / ERPNext | Self-host or Frappe Cloud; admissions, fees, assessments, portals             |

**Where Hogwarts diverges from the peer set:**

- **Multi-tenant SaaS by default, not a per-school install.** Every peer above is architected as one deployment per institution. Hogwarts is `schoolId`-scoped with subdomain routing — one deployment serves many schools, which is what makes a free tier and a 24-hour onboarding possible.
- **Modern stack, not PHP/Rails/Odoo.** Next.js 16 + React 19 + Prisma + TypeScript is what a 2026 contributor already knows.
- **Arabic-first rather than Arabic-translated.** No peer above treats RTL as the default rendering path.
- **Where they are ahead:** install-base maturity, plugin ecosystems (Odoo/Frappe especially), and years of edge cases in state reporting. Do not claim parity on breadth of deployment history.

### Design lessons

- **Fast = adopt a blueprint, or prompt an AI.** SchoolDeck/Testmate hit < 2 minutes by pre-baking the curriculum blueprint; AI tools hit 2–3 minutes from a topic prompt. The multi-step configuration wizard is the slow path teachers abandon.
- **Teachers skip configuration.** Per-question-type difficulty and Bloom distributions are almost always defaulted or skipped — design for "click through with sensible defaults," not "configure everything."
- **2–3 fixed professional layouts suffice.** Pixel-level header/footer/cover customization is Canva's job, not assessment software's; it is the over-engineering to avoid.
- **Print-ready PDF with branding set once is non-negotiable** for MENA/Sudan, where paper exams dominate.
- **A platform-curated, curriculum-aligned library is the moat.** AssessPrep's 60k IGCSE bank and SchoolDeck's baked-in CBSE blueprints are why schools pay on day one. Our exam catalog/adopt already proves this pattern.

**The gap nobody fills:** professional **Arabic-RTL exam papers** and **bilingual report cards**. No researched tool handles Arabic-primary paper formatting well — this is our regional advantage.

## Differentiators

- **Arabic-first, RTL-native** — every screen designed for Arabic first, English second.
- **Open source** — schools can audit the code; community contributes; no vendor lock-in.
- **Multi-tenant by design** — `schoolId`-scoped queries, subdomain routing, complete data isolation per school.
- **Region-priced** — free tier up to 100 students; Pro $1.50/student/month; Enterprise $1.00/student/month.
- **MEA payment rails** — Stripe + bankak + tap + cash + bank transfer + mobile money.
- **AI-assisted admission** — document classification, merit ranking, bank-receipt extraction.
