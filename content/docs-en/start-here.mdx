## Why it pays

A new person who knows where things live sells and builds in their first week, not their first month. Everything you need is on these Team pages. Everything we do is recorded in three places: the group, the CRM, and GitHub. If it is not there, it did not happen, and it does not count toward your share.

## The four words

| Word                  | Means                                                |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| «أنا»                 | I take this task. Add the day you will finish it.    |
| «عائق:»               | I am stuck, and this is what I need.                 |
| «اعتراض:»             | I disagree with a page or a rule, and this is why.   |
| «🧭 مكتب التشغيل»     | A message from the CEO office. Read it the same day. |

## Your week

- **Sunday:** read the plan and answer it with your task.
- **Tuesday:** one line: done, doing, or stuck.
- **Thursday:** the review. Bring done, not done, and what you need.

## Your first week

1. Get your accounts, then open the demo on balqalam.com on your phone.
2. Read [Team](/docs/team) to see who owns what, and who to ask.
3. Read [Vision](/docs/vision), [Shared economy](/docs/shared-economy), [CEO](/docs/ceo) and [Sprint](/docs/sprint).
4. Take one task from your department in the sprint. Write «أنا» under it.
5. Then read the pages for your work: sales reads Sales, CRM and Pricing; marketing reads Marketing; tech reads Support desk.

## When this happens

- **You don't know who to ask** → the [Team](/docs/team) table. Still unsure? Ask in the group.
- **You have no task by Tuesday** → take the next open task in your department, or help sales.
- **An account does not work** → tell Abdout the same day.
- **You did work nobody can see** → put it in the group, the CRM or GitHub. Then it counts.

## What you do

Take your first task this week, and write «أنا» under it.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** five pages before the first task is a lot for someone who wants to start now.
- **What would change it:** a new person who has no task after their first week. Then the first task comes before the reading.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: start-here — your reason» in the group.
