## The direction

Set by Abobakar, August 2026. Read this before you plan anything.

- **Cash first.** Every effort and every cost must bring a new school, keep an existing one, or generate a lead. If it does none of those, stop doing it.
- **The name is balqalam** (بالقلم), on **balqalam.com**. Not Hogwarts.
- **We are no longer positioning as open source.** Do not lead with the repo, the licence, or the community.
- **Paid ads are on.** Facebook, Instagram and TikTok — pages first, then paid. Older pages say we don't buy ads until 2027. That is void.
- **The goal is one irresistible offer**, two pages long, built from the real fears schools have.
- **One link** holding everything a school owner, a parent, or an investor needs — plus a printable version of the same thing.
- **Two videos.**

## What balqalam is

A school runs its whole operation on it. Admissions, students, classes, timetable, attendance, exams and report cards, fees, parents, messaging — one system, in Arabic, with every school's data separated from every other school's.

Arabic is the **default** language, not a translation layer added later. That matters more than it sounds: it is the reason a school in Khartoum or Riyadh can use it on day one without an English-speaking administrator.

| What a school gets            | In plain terms                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admissions**                | Parents apply on the school's own web address, and check their status later without making an account. Documents are read and sorted automatically. One click turns an accepted applicant into an enrolled student. |
| **Attendance**                | Register in seconds. Absence letters, excuses and follow-up happen on their own.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Exams & grades**            | Schedule exams, build papers that print properly in Arabic, mark them, and produce report cards and transcripts. Certificates carry a code anyone can verify online.                                                |
| **Fees & finance**            | Fee plans with instalments, invoices, receipts, refunds, discounts and scholarships — and real bookkeeping underneath.                                                                                              |
| **Timetable**                 | Drag periods into place. It refuses to double-book a teacher or a room.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Parent portal**             | A parent opens their phone and sees attendance, grades, report cards, fees and messages for each child.                                                                                                             |
| **Messaging**                 | Announcements to the whole school or one class, real-time chat, and **WhatsApp** delivery.                                                                                                                          |
| **Online courses**            | Lessons, video, progress tracking, completion certificates.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Transport**                 | Routes and stops, with parents notified when a child boards and gets off.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Library**                   | Catalogue and borrowing.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Setup**                     | The school sends the Excel list it already keeps. We do the rest — **same day**. Arabic column headers are fine.                                                                                                    |
| **Regulator reporting**       | Daily attendance submission to Abu Dhabi's ADEK, and the 2-hour parent-contact rule handled automatically.                                                                                                          |
| **Staff & payroll** `partial` | Staff records, salaries, payslips and timesheets exist, but this is not our strongest area. Lead with fees, mention payroll second.                                                                                 |

**The regulator line is worth your attention.** No competitor we have studied submits attendance to a regulator automatically. It is the single hardest thing for anyone to copy, and it is currently mentioned almost nowhere in our marketing.

Depth on any of these: <a href="/en/docs/mvp">what actually exists, feature by feature</a>.

## What it costs

| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Pro**        | **$1.50 per student / month**, minimum **$30/month** | Unlimited students and teachers. 20% off if paid yearly.       |

There is also a **14-day trial** of Pro with no card required.

<Callout>
  **The MENA-10 pilot is a separate thing — never mix it with the price list.**
  Ten schools, six months completely free on the live product with full support,
  in exchange for weekly feedback and permission to publish their story. It
  converts afterwards at $1.05 per student per month. One slot is taken.
</Callout>

Two things to hold onto:

- **The $30/month floor is real.** A 40-student school on Pro pays $30, not $60. This page is the only doc that says so — the number lives in the pricing code.
- **Self-serve online checkout is not open yet.** Every ad and every post must end in _book a demo_, _message us on WhatsApp_, or _apply for a pilot_. Never "buy now".

### Never claim

Each of these appears somewhere in our own material and is wrong, unmeasured, or not built. The right-hand column is what to write instead.

| Do not say                                                                   | Say this instead                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "80% time saved", "60% cost reduction", "25% more enrolment", "99.9% uptime" | Nothing. These are placeholders nobody measured. Describe what the software does instead of what it achieves.                            |
| "Our paying customers…"                                                      | "Our pilot school." We have no paying customers yet.                                                                                     |
| "Saves 30 admin hours a month"                                               | "The register takes seconds instead of a paper round." Describe the task, not a figure.                                                  |
| "12,000 students across 8 schools"                                           | "One school live, nine pilot slots open."                                                                                                |
| "Hijri calendar support"                                                     | Nothing — it does not exist.                                                                                                             |
| "Works offline"                                                              | "Built for slow connections." A true offline mode is planned, not shipped.                                                               |
| "Download our app"                                                           | "Opens in any browser and installs to your phone's home screen." There is no app-store app.                                              |
| "Advanced analytics"                                                         | "Reports on attendance, grades and fees." The analytics dashboard isn't finished.                                                        |
| "Six payment gateways"                                                       | "Records cash, bank transfer and card payments in one place. Online card payments are live through Stripe; local rails are being added." |

If you want a number that isn't in the safe list, ask before you publish it. A school owner who catches one invented figure stops believing the rest.

## Competitors

The study is already done and it is good — 356 lines, checked in August 2026, plus a full teardown of twenty competitor homepages and a bank of every question a school asks. Read it rather than starting again.

Two findings that should change how you write:

> Arabic-first is real competition here, not a gap. Madrasati, Noon, Edraak and Alef all render Arabic correctly. Our Arabic advantage holds against global systems and against ported foreign software — **it does not hold against Classera or Alef. In those rooms, lead with architecture and price, not with "we support Arabic."**

> Free national platforms set the floor. In Saudi, "Madrasati is free" is the first objection. The answer is administration — fees, payroll, transport, admissions — which national learning platforms do not do.

## Your turn

Below is a first draft of the decisions, not a blank form. Disagree with it — that's faster than starting from nothing. Copy this section into your own document and change what you want.

### Pick a tagline

It has to land on a school owner in three seconds. No "platform", no "solution", no "digital transformation".

|     | Arabic                               | English                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| A   | المدرسة كلها في مكان واحد            | The whole school, in one place            |
| B   | من الدفتر إلى بالقلم                 | From the ledger to balqalam               |
| C   | القبول والحضور والرسوم في شاشة واحدة | Admissions, attendance, fees — one screen |

My vote is **A** for the pages and **C** for ads, because C names the three things an owner is actually paying someone to do. B is the cleverest and the riskiest — it only works if people already know the name.

### The three fears to build the offer around

These are the six Abobakar listed, ranked by what actually stops a sale. Change the ranking if you disagree — whatever you put in the top three becomes the second page of the offer and the first three ad sets.

| Rank  | The fear                                                   | What answers it                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **It costs too much / free alternatives exist**            | Free forever under 100 students. $30/month for a small school. Six months free on the pilot. And: free national platforms don't do fees, payroll or admissions.                                                 |
| **2** | **When and how do we start? We can't handle a migration.** | They send the Excel file they already have. We do everything else. Running the same day. Arabic column headers are fine. This is our strongest answer — it turns their biggest fear into our biggest advantage. |
| **3** | **What if you disappear?**                                 | Every record exports to CSV or JSON at any moment. No lock-in, no hostage data.                                                                                                                                 |
| 4     | Security and privacy                                       | Each school's data is separated. Worth knowing: a large US competitor leaked around 60 million student records in 2025 — schools are asking this now.                                                           |
| 5     | Who supports us?                                           | Direct support during the pilot, from the people who built it.                                                                                                                                                  |
| 6     | What about parents and students?                           | Parents see everything on their phone. Applicants check their status without an account. This is usually excitement, not fear — use it as a closing line, not an objection.                                     |

### Five decisions only you can make

1. **How much per month on ads, and which platform first?** No budget is set. The old ≤$200 cap is void.
2. **Arabic only, or Arabic and English?** The product's default is Arabic. Two languages doubles the production work.
3. **Which country first — Sudan, Saudi, or UAE?** This changes who you're arguing against. In Saudi it's Madrasati and Classera; in the UAE the regulator story is the lead.
4. **Who appears on camera?** Both videos are blocked until this is answered.
5. **What is the single main button on the one link** — book a demo, apply for a pilot, or WhatsApp us? Only one can be primary.
