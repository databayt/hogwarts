## Why it pays

A one-page contract signed in a day is money a month sooner. Plain words remove the reason to send it to a lawyer, and one contract for everyone leaves nothing to negotiate.

## What the contract says

| Part         | In plain words                                                                    |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Service      | The whole Balqalam platform at the school's own address, with support and updates |
| Free month   | The first month costs nothing                                                     |
| Price        | $6.40 per student a year, counted on each payment date                            |
| Payment      | 50% when the free month ends, then 12.5% every three months                       |
| How to pay   | Sudanese pounds at the bank rate on the day, by Bankak, to the account on the invoice |
| Data         | The school owns its data and can export it any time; we keep it private           |
| Length       | One year, renewed unless either side says no 30 days before the end               |
| Late payment | After 30 days late and a written notice, we may pause access; the data is kept    |

Each clause answers a question schools ask before they sign: what do we get, what does it cost, and who owns our data?

## How to fill it

1. The school's name, who signs, and the number of students.
2. The start date and the day the free month ends.
3. The amounts — take them from the Pricing page.
4. Abdout signs for Databayt.

## When this happens

- **A school asks to change a clause** → ask what worries them, and write the worry on the deal. Most worries are already answered by a clause: data, length, late payment. Anything else goes to Abdout.
- **"Who owns our data?"** → the school does. It can export the data any time, and we keep it private.
- **A school wants to stop** → the contract runs a year and renews unless either side says no 30 days before the end. Tell Abdout the same day.
- **Payment is 30 days late** → the late-payment clause applies, and Abdout sends the written notice. The reminders before that are on [Monetize](/docs/monetize).

## What you do

- Never change a clause yourself. Send any change the school asks for to Abdout.
- Put the signed copy in the CRM the same day.

## References

Where this page's way of working comes from:

- U.S. Plain Language Action and Information Network, [Federal Plain Language Guidelines](https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/) — short sentences, everyday words, "you" and "we". A contract both sides can read in one sitting.
- Fisher, Ury & Patton (2011), [*Getting to Yes*, 3rd ed.](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/getting-to-yes/) — agree on objective criteria instead of bargaining over positions. The amount comes from the public price, not from a negotiation.
