**The problem: schools run on paper, Excel and WhatsApp. Our answer: Balqalam puts the whole school in one place, in Arabic.**

## Why it pays

A pitch is the first ten seconds of every sale. When everyone says the same thing the same way, every conversation starts where the last one ended, and a school hears one clear company.

## To a school owner

"Admissions, timetable, attendance, live classes and fees — in one system, in Arabic, on any phone. We set it up with you. Your first month is free; after that it is $6.40 per student a year."

## To a partner

"You know the schools; we have the platform. Bring a school and earn half of its first-year payments, for each of your first five schools."

## To an investor

"A school platform built for Arabic-speaking schools, starting in Sudan, where our first school teaches on it this month. We grow one paid step at a time: ten paying schools first, then more curricula and more countries."

## Why us

- Built for Arabic schools first, not translated later.
- The Sudanese curriculum is already inside: grades 1 to 12.
- We set the school up with them; they are never alone.

Each line answers what a school compares us with: a foreign system translated late, or no system at all.

## When this happens

- **You have ten seconds** → say the problem and our answer (the line at the top), then ask: "May I show you on your phone?"
- **"Who else uses it?"** → our first school, by name only with its permission, and the Sudanese curriculum inside.
- **You are asked something you don't know** → "Good question. I'll confirm and come back to you today." Then ask in the group.
- **A partner or an investor asks** → use their pitch, then follow [Partnership](/docs/partners) or [Investors](/docs/investors).

## What you do

Know the school pitch by heart. Use the other two when you are asked.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** "everything in one place" sounds like every competitor. Leading with one sharp pain, such as fees, may land harder.
- **What would change it:** demos that keep stopping on the same feature. Then the pitch leads with that feature.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: pitch — your reason» in the group.
