**Money that does not cost us a share of the company comes first.**

## The ladder

1. **Schools** — the price, paid by schools. Always first.
2. **Free credits** — cloud and AI programmes (Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, Neon).
3. **Grants and competitions** — money for impact in education.
4. **Investors** — only after ten paying schools.
