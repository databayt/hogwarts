## Why it pays

Free credits and grants cut our costs or add cash without giving away part of the company. About $500 of credits is a month of the costs on [Monetize](/docs/monetize) that we don't pay. Raising after ten paying schools gets a better price: investors pay more for proof than for plans. Until then, every hour spent on investors is an hour not spent on schools.

## The ladder

1. **Schools** — the price, paid by schools. Always first.
2. **Free credits** — cloud and AI programmes (Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, Neon).
3. **Grants and competitions** — money for impact in education.
4. **Investors** — only after ten paying schools.

The order is the cost of the money. School revenue costs nothing, credits cost an application, grants cost weeks of writing, and investors cost a share of the company.

## Before you apply

- Check the deadline and who owns it.
- Reuse what we have: the pitch on [Sales](/docs/sales), the numbers, the [Vision](/docs/vision).
- Tell Abdout before you submit anything in the company's name.

## What we tell investors

"A school platform built for Arabic-speaking schools, starting in Sudan, where our first school teaches on it this month. We grow one paid step at a time: ten paying schools first, then more curricula and more countries."

- **Where we are:** a working platform (admissions, timetable, attendance, live classes, exams, fees, messages), our first school's first payment due 19 December, and a small team with no salaries yet.
- **What money would buy:** salespeople in Sudan and Saudi Arabia, faster setup for school groups, more curricula.
- **What they see, every month:** paying schools, students, monthly revenue. The same three numbers each time build trust. Only Abdout shares them.

## When this happens

- **A programme needs a registered company or a partner letter** → tell Abdout first. The company papers are with him.
- **A deadline clashes with the sprint** → the sprint's school goals come first. Thursday's review decides whether the application goes ahead.
- **An application is rejected** → ask for the feedback, keep the text, and reuse it for the next one.
- **An investor asks for numbers or a deck** → Abdout sends them. Meanwhile, share the pitch and the demo.
- **Someone offers money now** → thank them, take their contact, and send it to Abdout the same day. Don't discuss terms.

## What you do

- Found a programme? Post it in the group with its link and its deadline.
- Only Abdout talks numbers or terms with investors. Send any investor contact to him the same day.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** a grant can take weeks of writing for money that may never come. And waiting for ten paying schools may be too slow: money now could pay the salespeople who get us there.
- **What would change it:** an application that needs more than a week of one person's time for less than three months of our costs — we skip it. A forecast that we run out of money before the tenth paying school — we raise earlier, and Abdout decides.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: fundraising — your reason» in the group.
