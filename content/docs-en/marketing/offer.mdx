## The short offer

### Page one — what does the school get?

- Admissions, attendance, fees, results and communication in one place.
- An Arabic interface from day one.
- Set up from the school's current Excel file.
- Clear portals for admin, teacher, family and student.
- A published price with no setup fee or hidden modules — pending final commercial sign-off.

### Page two — the three fears

| Fear                      | Answer                                                    |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cost                      | Free for small schools, a clear price for larger ones     |
| Migration                 | The school sends its current data; our team does the setup |
| Vendor disappears / lock-in | CSV/JSON export, and the school owns its data           |

## The MENA-10 pilot

- Ten schools only.
- Six months free on the live product, with support.
- In return for weekly feedback and permission to publish the school's story.
- Converts afterwards at $1.05 / student / month.
- One seat taken: King Fahad Schools, Khartoum.
- **Never mix the pilot offer into the public price list.**

## Lead qualification

Asked on WhatsApp, after a short first reply:

1. School name and country.
2. The person's role in the decision.
3. Approximate number of students.
4. How operations run today.
5. The biggest problem they want solved.
6. When they want to start.
7. Who else is part of the decision.

A like or a follow is **not a lead**. Full definition in [Measurement](/docs/marketing/measurement).
