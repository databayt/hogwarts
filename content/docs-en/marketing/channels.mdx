## Each platform's role

| Platform  | Role                                                                 | Cadence            | Rules                                                                                 |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Facebook  | Reaching school owners and principals; stories, carousels, objections, click-to-WhatsApp ads | 3–4 posts / week | Medium-length copy, short videos, carousels, real situations. First ad only after content proves itself organically |
| Instagram | Visual identity, product screens, Reels, saves and shares           | 3 posts or Reels / week | Stories for questions, polls and behind-the-scenes. One saveable idea per carousel |
| TikTok    | Reach, testing education and human hooks                            | 2–3 videos / week  | A real person on camera or a screen recording with a human voice, mostly 20–60 s. No spend until we know which video attracts real decision-makers |
| LinkedIn  | B2B trust, school networks, procurement, partners and investors     | 2 posts / week     | Deeper analysis, founder view, building the product, pilot stories. Never a verbatim copy of Facebook |
| WhatsApp  | Qualification, demo booking, follow-up                              | Per conversation   | Never random broadcast. A short first reply, then the [qualification questions](/docs/marketing/offer). Every qualified conversation goes into the CRM |

## The weekly repurposing cycle

| Day             | Original idea                         | Repurposed into                                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Sunday          | Learning science / trusted news       | Reel + TikTok + LinkedIn insight                    |
| Tuesday         | A school-operations problem           | Facebook post + Instagram carousel                  |
| Thursday        | Proof from the product                | Screen recording + Story + WhatsApp follow-up asset |
| All week        | Question / reply / behind the scenes  | Stories and comments                                |

Three original ideas a week are enough for **8–12 distributed pieces**.
