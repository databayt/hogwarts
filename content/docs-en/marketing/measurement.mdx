## North Star

**Qualified demos booked per month.**

## Qualified lead — definition

A lead is qualified when it has:

- A real school.
- A country and an approximate student count.
- A person with a role in the decision, or access to one.
- A problem balqalam can solve.
- Intent, or a reasonable timeline to evaluate.

## Funnel metrics

| Stage         | Metric                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Content       | Reach inside the target audience, video completion, saves, shares, comment quality |
| Interest      | Link visits, WhatsApp opens, demo views                             |
| Qualification | Qualified leads ÷ all leads                                         |
| Demo          | Demos booked, show-up rate                                          |
| Pilot         | Demo → pilot                                                        |
| Revenue       | Pilot → paid, MRR attributed to source                              |

## Numbers we never chase on their own

Followers · impressions · likes · the cheapest cost per lead regardless of quality · views from students when the target is the school owner.

## UTM convention

```
utm_source=facebook|instagram|tiktok|linkedin
utm_medium=organic|paid|retargeting
utm_campaign=country_angle_month
utm_content=hook_format_version
```

Example: `utm_campaign=sudan_excel_migration_2026_09` · `utm_content=send_excel_reel_v1`

## Monthly review

1. Which topic brought the highest **quality**, not the highest reach?
2. Which persona responded?
3. What was the most repeated objection?
4. Where does the funnel lose people?
5. Is our WhatsApp reply fast?
6. Do people show up to booked demos?
7. What content helped a sale even without producing a lead directly?
8. What do we stop next month?
