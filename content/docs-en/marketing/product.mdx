## What is balqalam?

An integrated Arabic system that runs a school end to end — from admissions to reports, fees and communication with families.

- **Arabic is the default language**, not a translation layer added later.
- **Every school's data is separated** from every other school's.

## What does the customer get?

Use the "safe marketing description" as written, or close to it — never add promises on top.

| Area                 | Safe marketing description                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Admissions           | A parent applies from the school's link and tracks the application without creating an account; an accepted applicant becomes an enrolled student |
| Attendance           | Taking attendance and following up on absences and excuses                                     |
| Exams & grades       | Schedules, Arabic exam papers, marking, certificates and reports                               |
| Fees & finance       | Fee plans, instalments, invoices, receipts, discounts and scholarships                         |
| Timetable            | Building the timetable while preventing teacher or room clashes                                |
| Family portal        | Attendance, grades, reports, fees and messages for every child                                 |
| Messaging            | Announcements, conversations and delivery channels including WhatsApp, depending on setup     |
| E-learning           | Lessons, video, progress tracking and completion certificates                                  |
| Transport            | Routes, stops and tracking students boarding and alighting                                     |
| Library              | Cataloguing and lending                                                                        |
| Setup                | The school sends its current Excel file; our team prepares and imports it                      |
| Staff & payroll      | Partially there — never the opening of a pitch                                                 |
