## Ideal customer (ICP)

A private Arabic K-12 school or school network that runs admissions, attendance, fees and results, and depends partly or fully on **paper, Excel and WhatsApp** — or on several systems that don't talk to each other.

## Personas

| Persona                     | Wants                                         | Fears                                              | Message                                                        |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| School owner                | Control and financial visibility              | Cost, failure, the vendor folding, data lock-in    | The whole school in front of you, a clear price, and your data stays yours |
| Principal / general manager | Less chaos, oversight of every department     | Staff pushback, teaching disrupted by the move     | We start from your existing Excel file and move you over step by step |
| Admissions & registration   | No more retyping family data                  | Errors and lost follow-ups                         | The parent applies once and the data arrives organised for review |
| Finance officer             | What's due, what's late, receipts, discounts  | Lost invoices                                      | Fees, invoices and receipts in one ledger                      |
| IT / procurement            | Answers on isolation, export, hosting, roles, integration | Promises that don't hold                 | Precise answers — no invented certifications or compliance     |

**Rule:** one persona per piece. Never address the owner and the teacher in the same copy.

## Influencers we don't sell to directly

Teachers · parents · education consultants · the EdTech community. They create the trust signal that makes a decision-maker respond, but content is not written to them as buyers.

## Market order

| Phase | Market       | Goal                                                       | Strengths                                         | Risks & objections                                                              |
| ----- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Sudan        | Fill the pilot from warm relationships, build real case studies | Context, network, a live school we can name   | Ability to pay in dollars, infrastructure, the economy                          |
| 2     | Saudi Arabia | Larger private schools, higher revenue                     | —                                                 | "My school's platform is free", Classera, local payment, data residency, procurement |
| 3     | UAE          | Schools looking for operations and compliance              | —                                                 | ADEK is not the lead message until the live link, hosting and legal requirements are confirmed |

In Saudi Arabia we don't advertise broadly until there is an official answer on hosting and payment.

## Targeting rule

Knowledge content can travel across the Arab world, but **the ad, the landing page and the sales offer are built for one country per campaign**.
