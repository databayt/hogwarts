## Stages

| Stage       | What happens                                                                                       |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Idea     | From a customer question, an objection, news, research, product use or the idea inbox. Tie it to a business goal |
| 2. Research | At least one trusted source. Record what the evidence says and doesn't. Check the [research policy](/docs/marketing/content) |
| 3. Brief    | Audience, country, funnel stage, problem, one message, product bridge, CTA, KPI — on the content-brief template |
| 4. Draft    | Core copy first, then derive the platform versions. A clear visual direction — never "a nice image" |
| 5. Review   | Scientific/source review, product truth, language and identity, CTA, link and UTM                  |
| 6. Publish  | Publish on schedule, answer relevant comments, move any sales question to WhatsApp or the CRM     |
| 7. Measure  | At 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days. Record the *kind* of audience, not just the number. Turn objections into new ideas |

## Definition of done

A piece isn't ready unless it has:

- [ ] One persona.
- [ ] One idea.
- [ ] A documented source wherever there's a claim.
- [ ] A product claim allowed by the [Claim Registry](/docs/marketing/claims).
- [ ] One CTA.
- [ ] A visual direction or shot list.
- [ ] A specific platform version.
- [ ] A UTM wherever there's a link.
- [ ] An expected KPI.

## Idea inbox

Drop any raw idea without tidying it, under: **raw ideas · customer questions · objections from sales calls · news and research to review · product moments worth a video**. At the weekly review, good ideas move to the [editorial calendar](/docs/marketing/channels) or get a content brief.
