---
title: 90-Day Plan
description: Four phases with exit gates — foundation and assets, editorial launch, product proof and demand, then the paid test — and the day-90 review.
---

**The outcome:** a marketing system that produces trustworthy content steadily, turns interest into qualified demos, and gives a safe base for running ads without waste or misleading claims.

## Phase 1 — Foundation & assets (weeks 1–2)

**Week 1 — Close the decisions**

- Approve the first market, the language and tone, the slogan and the primary button.
- Choose the video presenter and the test budget.
- Review the [Claim Registry](/docs/marketing/claims) and settle the P0 conflicts.
- **Output:** a brief signed off by product and marketing.

**Week 2 — Sales assets**

- Write the [two-page offer](/docs/marketing/offer).
- Build the single link.
- Crop and standardise 12–20 Arabic screenshots.
- Record a two-minute tour video and a 60-second offer video.
- Prepare the WhatsApp opening message and the qualification questions.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Exit gate:** no ad before there is a link, a video, a CTA and a CRM.
</Callout>

## Phase 2 — Editorial launch (weeks 3–4)

**Week 3 — First batch**

- Produce three original ideas from different pillars; turn them into 8–12 assets.
- Create the [UTM naming convention](/docs/marketing/measurement).
- Prepare the publishing and review calendar.
- Test WhatsApp response speed.

**Week 4 — Organic measurement**

- Publish the second batch.
- Log the quality of comments and the job roles of people engaging.
- Pick the best 3 hooks.
- Hold 3–5 discovery conversations from the warm network.
- Rewrite messages in the customers' own words.

<Callout>
  **Exit gate:** content that attracts a decision-maker or starts a relevant
  conversation.
</Callout>

## Phase 3 — Product proof & demand (weeks 5–8)

| Week | Theme                          | Product proof                    | Objection                        | CTA                      |
| ---- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 5    | Moving off Excel               | Import, or a populated dashboard | "Will we retype everything?"     | Book a demo              |
| 6    | Attendance as an early signal  | Taking attendance and follow-up  | "Our teachers aren't technical"  | Watch the tour           |
| 7    | Fees without scattered ledgers | Invoice, receipt and fee plan    | Cost and free platforms          | See the price or WhatsApp |
| 8    | Data ownership and trust       | Export and roles                 | "What if the company disappears?" | Book a Q&A session      |

**End of week 8:** review the best topic, format and CTA · decide whether ads are ready · no case study with results before measurement and approval.

## Phase 4 — Paid test & optimisation (weeks 9–12)

| Campaign | Message                                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| A        | "Send your Excel and start from the data you have"      |
| B        | "Under 100 students? Free, permanently"                 |
| C        | "Admissions, attendance and fees on one screen"         |

**Initial budget split, once the budget is set:** 60% generating conversations or demos · 25% retargeting video viewers and link visitors · 15% testing new creative.

**Every week:**

- Review qualified leads, not raw leads.
- Listen to sales calls or read their summaries.
- Stop creative that brings the wrong audience.
- Write a post answering the most repeated objection.
- Update [Measurement](/docs/marketing/measurement).

## Day-90 review

- Number of qualified leads.
- Demos booked and held.
- Demo → pilot.
- Cost per qualified demo.
- Top 5 topics by audience quality; bottom 5 and why.
- New objections.
- Claims that need measuring in the product.
- **Next quarter's call:** expand, hold, or change market.
