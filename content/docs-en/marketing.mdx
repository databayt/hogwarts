## The strategic decision

balqalam will not show up as a software page that only posts its features, and it will not become a general education-news magazine. We are building **a smart Arabic reference for school owners and principals** — one that explains how a school learns better and runs more clearly, then proves how balqalam turns that knowledge into daily work.

## Vision

For balqalam to be the clearest Arabic reference for a principal who wants to understand how a modern school works, and how to move from paper and scattered files to integrated, measurable management.

**What we do not want to become:**

- A feature page and nothing else.
- A general education-news magazine with no link to the decision-maker.
- A study-tips account aimed at students.
- A page chasing every education trend.
- An account that inflates neuroscience or spreads neuromyths.
- A brand that claims results it never measured.

**The editorial promise:** we explain complex knowledge that affects schools in plain language, turn it into an administrative decision, then show the practical application when balqalam is part of the answer.

## The eight principles

| #   | Principle                  | Meaning                                                   |
| --- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Cash first                 | Content serves trust, a lead, or retention                |
| 2   | Problem before feature     | Start from the school's day, not the module's name        |
| 3   | Show before claim          | A screen recording beats a marketing adjective            |
| 4   | Evidence before excitement | Keep the study, the interpretation and the claim apart    |
| 5   | One audience per piece     | Never address the principal, teacher and student at once  |
| 6   | One action per piece       | No three competing CTAs                                   |
| 7   | Arabic-first               | Arabic copy is original, not a literal translation        |
| 8   | Country-aware              | A Saudi objection differs from a Sudanese or Emirati one  |

## The 90-day goal

- A trustworthy foundation for publishing and advertising.
- Turn the product from a feature list into a story people understand.
- Produce **36 core ideas** that can be repurposed.
- Book qualified demos — not just grow followers.
- Collect the market's own language from comments and calls, and rewrite our messages with it.

**North Star:** qualified demos booked per month, from content and ads. Detail in [Measurement](/docs/marketing/measurement).
