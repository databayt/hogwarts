**One message: Balqalam runs your whole school in one place, in Arabic — and your first month is free.**

## Why it pays

Marketing fills the top of [Sales](/docs/sales): every post should make a school owner message us. We count conversations started, not likes.

## Who we talk to

- School owners and principals in Sudan first.
- School groups with branches before single schools.

One clear audience lets one message do the work.

## Where

| Place                              | Who runs it |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Balqalam's Facebook page           | Sedon       |
| School-owner WhatsApp communities  | Huwaida     |
| Short videos of one feature        | Moed        |

## What we post each week

- One real story from a school, with its permission.
- One short video of one feature.
- One practical tip for principals.

The same three, every week. Being seen often and in the same way matters more than being clever once.

## Say, don't say

| Say                                                               | Don't say                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Admissions, timetable, attendance, live classes, fees — one place | "Works offline"                       |
| In Arabic and English                                             | "Native app"                          |
| Your first month is free                                          | "99.9% uptime", "guaranteed results"  |
| Your data is yours                                                | Any number we cannot prove            |
| Balqalam (بالقلم) — the name schools know                         | "Hogwarts" — our name inside the team |

## When this happens

- **A school agrees to share its story** → get the yes in writing (WhatsApp is fine), with what may be shown. No student's name or face without it.
- **A post gets questions** → answer within the school day, and pass the person to sales that day, in the CRM.
- **Someone wants a claim we cannot prove** → it stays in the "Don't say" column.
- **A post works** → reuse it in the communities, and make it next week's video.

## What you do

- **Moed** writes and films, **Sedon** posts, **Huwaida** shares in the communities.
- Check every post against "Say, don't say" before it goes out.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** three posts a week may be too few to be noticed, or too many for a team that should be selling.
- **What would change it:** a kind of post that starts no conversation in four weeks. Its time moves to the WhatsApp communities.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: marketing — your reason» in the group.
