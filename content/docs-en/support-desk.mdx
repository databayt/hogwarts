**A school that waits for an answer stops trusting us. Our standard: an answer the same school day.**

## Why it pays

A school pays its first 50% after the free month, and only if that month went well. Support is where the free month is won: every answer on the same school day is part of the sale.

## Where problems come in

| Channel                              | Who watches                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| The school's WhatsApp group with us  | The person on duty (this sprint: Sedon) |
| «Report an issue» inside the app     | Abdout and Kun                       |
| The help center (/docs/support)      | Schools help themselves              |

## What the person on duty does

1. Answer the school within the school day.
2. A bug? Send it with «Report an issue»: a screenshot and the steps.
3. Tell the school when it is fixed.

## The free month: week one

Tech sets up every new school the same way, so nothing is missed:

1. The school and its admin login. Check that the admin can sign in.
2. Students, teachers and classes, imported from the school's own Excel.
3. The timetable, then attendance and live classes for the first week.
4. Each role learns to sign in: send the help-center guides for signing in, adding a student, and attendance.
5. On day 7, ask the school one question: "What is not working?" Fix that first.

## What we never do

- Change a school's data without the school asking.
- Promise a feature or a date. Say: "We'll confirm and come back to you."

## When this happens

- **A school is angry** → answer first, explain later: "We see it, we're on it, and we'll update you by [time]." Then keep that time.
- **A school asks for a feature** → write it on the school's deal in the CRM and send it through «Report an issue» as a request. Promise nothing.
- **You cannot fix it today** → tell the school when you will update them, and send it to Kun through «Report an issue».
- **The same question comes twice** → it belongs in the help center. Tell Abdout which guide is missing.

## What you do

- **On duty:** read the school groups in the morning and in the afternoon.
- Everyone: point schools to the help center first.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** same-day answers from one person on duty will wear that person out as schools grow.
- **What would change it:** more than 10 school messages a day on duty. Then we add a second person, or a help-center guide for the top question.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: support-desk — your reason» in the group.
