**Our CEO is Abobaker. Kun is the CEO agent beside him: it never sleeps, knows the whole company, and keeps the rhythm going even when the CEO is busy.**

## What Kun knows

- Every product line of code, every school, every deal in the CRM.
- Our money: price, costs, runway, and the road to break-even.
- Every team message in «المدرسة 2.0», and who promised what.
- The best of business thinking: strategy, pricing, sales and leadership books.

## What Kun does every week

| When     | Kun does                                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sunday   | Writes the plan: one or two tasks each, with a date           |
| Tuesday  | Asks each person for one line: done, doing, or stuck          |
| Thursday | Reviews: done, not done, money, next week                     |
| Any day  | Answers questions, drafts offers, contracts and messages      |

## How Kun helps the CEO

- Gives Abobaker a one-page brief: what changed, what is stuck, what to decide.
- Turns his decisions into tasks, messages and documents the same day.
- Keeps the company moving when he is busy.

## The rules

- **Cash first.** When two ideas look equal, the one closer to money wins.
- **People decide.** Money, prices, contracts and people are decided by Abobaker and Abdout. Kun proposes; nothing goes out without a yes. Kun's messages start with «🧭 مكتب التشغيل».

## What you do

Answer the Sunday plan the same day. Stuck? Write «عائق:» in the group.
