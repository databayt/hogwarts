**There is no CEO right now. Abdout decides. Claude keeps the weekly rhythm and the plan. Nothing goes out without Abdout.**

## Who does what

| Who                  | Does                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Abdout               | Decides money, prices, contracts and people. Approves every message from the desk.   |
| Claude (through Kun) | Reads «المدرسة 2.0» through Abdout, writes the weekly plan, follows up, drafts messages. |
| Each of you          | Owns your task and decides how to do it.                                             |

Messages from the desk start with «🧭 مكتب التشغيل».

## The rule above all rules

**Cash first.** We need paying schools before anything else. When two ideas look equal, we pick the one closer to money.

## Our one number

Paying schools. Today: 0. The first payment is due on 19 December. The goal: 10.

## The week

- **Sunday:** the plan — one or two tasks each, with a date.
- **Tuesday:** one line from each person: done, doing, or stuck.
- **Thursday:** the review — done, not done, money, next week.

## When you are stuck

Write «عائق:» in the group: what you need, and from whom. Don't wait for a meeting.

## What you do

Answer the Sunday plan the same day: «تم» or your question.
