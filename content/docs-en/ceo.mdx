**The CEO office runs the company rhythm: it knows the whole company, sets the week, and keeps work moving.**

## What the office tracks

- Every product, every school, every deal in the CRM.
- Our money: price, costs, runway, and the road to break-even.
- Every team message in «المدرسة 2.0», and who promised what.
- The best of business thinking: strategy, pricing, sales and leadership.

## The week

| When     | The office                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sunday   | Writes the plan: one or two tasks each, with a date      |
| Tuesday  | Asks each person for one line: done, doing, or stuck     |
| Thursday | Reviews: done, not done, money, next week                |
| Any day  | Answers questions, drafts offers, contracts and messages |

## How decisions move

- A one-page brief: what changed, what is stuck, what to decide.
- Every decision becomes tasks, messages and documents the same day.
- Nothing waits for a meeting.

## The rules

- **Cash first.** When two ideas look equal, the one closer to money wins.
- **Approval before it goes out.** Money, prices, contracts and hiring need a yes before anything is sent. Office messages start with «🧭 مكتب التشغيل».

## What you do

Answer the Sunday plan the same day. Stuck? Write «عائق:» in the group.
