**We raise after ten paying schools. Until then, schools, grants and free credits fund us.**

## Why it pays

Raising after ten paying schools means a better price for the company: investors pay more for proof than for plans. Until then, every hour spent on investors is an hour not spent on schools.

## Where we are

- A working platform: admissions, timetable, attendance, live classes, exams, fees and messages.
- Our first school teaches on it this month; its first payment is due on 19 December.
- A small team that builds and sells, with no salaries yet.

## What money would buy

- Salespeople in Sudan and Saudi Arabia.
- Faster setup for school groups.
- More curricula (see the Vision page).

## When we raise

| Now                               | After ten paying schools                         |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Revenue, grants, free credits     | A seed round, from investors who know education  |

## What investors can see

- Every month: paying schools, students, monthly revenue.
- The product itself: the demo on balqalam.com.

Three numbers, every month, are enough to show the trend. Sharing the same three each time builds trust.

## When this happens

- **An investor asks for numbers** → the three monthly numbers are all we share, and Abdout shares them.
- **An investor asks for a deck** → Abdout sends it. Meanwhile, share the [Pitch](/docs/pitch) and the demo.
- **Someone offers money now** → thank them, take their contact, and send it to Abdout the same day. Don't discuss terms.

## What you do

- Only Abdout talks numbers or terms with investors.
- Send any investor contact to Abdout the same day.

## Argue with this page

- **The case against:** waiting for ten paying schools may be too slow. Money now could pay the salespeople who get us there.
- **What would change it:** a forecast that we run out of money before the tenth paying school. Then we raise earlier, and Abdout decides.
- **Disagree?** Write «اعتراض: investors — your reason» in the group.
