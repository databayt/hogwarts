**We earn one thing: $6.40 (24 SAR) per student per year, paid by the school.**

## The road to money

| Step                    | Means                                         | When            |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 1. First payment        | Our first school pays its first half          | 19 December     |
| 2. Three paying schools | Proof that schools pay                        | Sprint 4        |
| 3. Break-even           | About 940 paying students cover our costs     | ~10 schools     |
| 4. Ten paying schools   | We open the next step of the Vision           | After break-even |

## What it costs us

About $500 a month: servers, the AI tools we build with, and domains. We re-check it every month. At $0.53 per student per month, break-even is about 940 students.

## Where the money is

- Small schools prove the product.
- School groups with many branches pay the bills: one contract, many schools.

## How we bill (by hand, for now)

1. The free month ends: we send an invoice by WhatsApp and email.
2. The school pays by Bankak, in Sudanese pounds at the bank rate that day.
3. We mark the deal "Paid" in the CRM.

## What you do

Log every conversation about money in the CRM the same day.
