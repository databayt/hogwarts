## Why it pays

This page is the Drive in numbers: cash flow first, break-even or bust. Every other Team page is judged by one question: does it move a school from Trial to Paid sooner?

## The road to money

| Step                    | Means                                         | When            |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 1. First payment        | Our first school pays its first half          | 19 December     |
| 2. Three paying schools | Proof that schools pay                        | Sprint 4        |
| 3. Break-even           | About 940 paying students cover our costs     | ~10 schools     |
| 4. Ten paying schools   | We open the next step of the Vision           | After break-even |

Step 1 matters more than its size. The first paid invoice proves that schools pay, and it tests our billing from end to end.

## What it costs us

About $500 a month: servers, the AI tools we build with, and domains. We re-check it every month. At $0.53 per student per month, break-even is about 940 students.

The rule of thumb: **break-even students = monthly costs ÷ $0.53.** If costs rise to $600, break-even moves to about 1,130 students.

## Where the money is

- Small schools prove the product.
- School groups with many branches pay the bills: one contract, many schools.

A group with five branches is five schools for the work of one sale.

## How we bill (by hand, for now)

1. The free month ends: we send an invoice by WhatsApp and email.
2. The school pays by Bankak, in Sudanese pounds at the bank rate that day.
3. We mark the deal "Paid" in the CRM.

## When this happens

- **An invoice is late** → send a friendly reminder the next day, and log it on the deal. At 30 days late, the late-payment clause in the [Contract](/docs/contract) applies, and only Abdout sends the written notice.
- **A school pays part** → write the amount on the deal, thank them, and agree the date for the rest.
- **Costs go up** → the monthly re-check updates the cost and the break-even line on this page, the same day.
- **Someone proposes spending money** → ask what it brings in, and by when. No revenue line, no spend.
